PROSPECTIVE CORRECTION: CENTERED ACTION COSTS AND LINEAR STATE VALUE
2026-11-20 | Design for one bounded engineering gate; no research release

DECISION
Recommend implementing ONE isolated corrected baseline for mechanics testing:
the same compact network with centered branch costs and an unconstrained
linear scalar state-value component. This is an established dueling-style
correction,not a claim that centering,dueling DDQN or demonstration learning
is new. Its purpose is to remove the specifically observed outer-softplus
attenuation path and separate action preferences from scalar offsets. Actual
calibration,policy benefit,latency and thesis novelty remain unestablished.
No weights,criteria or results of the completed pilot are modified.

1. PRECISE MODEL
Retain533->24 ReLU->161 FP32 outputs. No extra head,width or hidden observation.
Outputs0..149 are raw vehicle costs c_i,d;150..159 are raw motion costs c_j,d;
output160 is a signed scalar V(s). Define scaled raw branch costs
  z_i,d=c_i,d/30 for vehicle branches i=0..29,
  z_j,d=c_j,d/2 for the two movement branches.
Center each branch over ALL five declared actions:
  A_b,d=z_b,d-(1/5)*sum_e z_b,e.
Define the same-action joint critic
  Q_theta(s,a)=V_theta(s)-sum_(b=0..31) A_theta,b,a_b(s).
This is a signed Q approximation. No outer softplus,clamp or forced Q<=0.
The true nonpositive reward-return convention remains unchanged;positive
predictions are possible approximation errors and must be reported,not hidden.
No horizon multiplier or terminal-anchor auxiliary loss is added.

The local feasibility screen and deterministic first-index ties remain the
same. For a nonterminal state choose independently
  a_b*=argmin_(d allowed by inherited screen) z_b,d.
Centering is independent of a chosen action and V is state-only,so this is
an exact masked maximum of THIS additive joint critic,not of the physical
coupled UAV-IoV problem. Q remains defined for every legal stored action,
even if its choice is excluded by the current screen. Center over all5actions,
not the changing masked subset,so the critic is not silently redefined when
the allowed set changes. No remote feasibility or battery filter is added.

At terminal next states there is no policy decoding and bootstrap0. The
unscaled network is not forced to output0 for arbitrary terminal inputs;
no terminal-network-value guarantee is claimed. Last physical state Q should
estimate its remaining reward,not be incorrectly compared with0.

2. WHAT THE CORRECTION DOES AND DOES NOT PROVE
For the selected action,dQ/dV=1. For raw cost z_b,e,
  dQ/dz_b,e=1/5-1[e=a_b].
For the actual network outputs,divide those derivatives by30or2 according
to branch type. There is no multiplier sigmoid(C) that becomes exponentially
small when the branch costs shift negative. ReLU/shared-feature behavior,
Huber residual saturation,gradient clipping and optimizer effects remain.
This is not a proof of globally nonvanishing parameter gradients or convergence.

Adding any common branch offset leaves both A and the selected action
unchanged,so it cannot shift Q through that branch's arbitrary mean. V provides
a separately represented scalar offset. The centered cost sum has zero mean
under uniform independent sampling of all declared actions at fixed s;in that
limited algebraic sense V is the mean of the modeled joint values. It is not
automatically the greedy policy value or a physically calibrated baseline.

For unrestricted additive linear outputs,centering is a parameterization
choice rather than additional expressive capacity. An uncentered linear
critic can represent the same joint functions by absorbing branch means into
its state-value output. Do not claim a unique new function class or attribute
all gains to centering. Removing the output nonlinearity and centering are
two distinct changes;the comparison below must distinguish them.

3. BC AND DDQN GRADIENT PATHS
BC uses the existing allowed-action cross entropy on -z/T,T=1,averaged over
vehicles plus the separate average over movement branches. Using centered
scores yields the same mathematical softmax probabilities;use raw scores in
implementation to avoid needless centering in the BC loss. Teacher labels
remain training-only and include stationary motion. No teacher Q regression.
The last-layer V row has no direct BC-loss gradient;shared hidden parameters
do receive BC gradients,so the numerical V(s) can still change during BC.
Do not say that V is frozen just because its final row is unsupervised.
No post-BC value-row reset,teacher-return fitting or extra supervised target
is added in this design. All initialization semantics must be paired explicitly.

After optional BC,discard its Adam state,initialize the target from the
post-BC online weights and create fresh DDQN Adam. For each replay transition,
  a_next=decode_theta(s_next),
  y=r+gamma*(1-done)*Q_target(s_next,a_next),gamma=1,
  L_TD=mean Huber_beta1(Q_online(s,a)-stop_gradient(y)).
Terminal rows bypass decode/target evaluation and use y=r. TD trains V,
advantages and shared features;target parameters receive no gradients/updates
except scheduled exact copies. A single optimizer step covers each TD batch.
No continuous imitation loss,reward change,prioritized replay or n-step
target is introduced. These choices are correction scope,not yet a full-run
optimizer/budget freeze. Historical pilot data may diagnose but cannot confirm.

4. COST AND STORAGE EXPECTATION
Same16,841parameters,67,364FP32 parameter bytes and inherited20buffer bytes.
No training-only teacher code or physical decoder is needed in deployment.
Same neural MACs as the old compact model;branch means and centering add
O(32*5) scalar work,while the outer scalar softplus is removed. For selected
Q,one may subtract the sum of branch means without materializing a full
centered tensor,provided equivalence is checked under the declared arithmetic.
No speedup is assumed. Complete validation,screen,forward,selection,Q if used,
and output copy remain inside matched action-selection measurements.
If deployment returns actions only,it may omit unused scalar evaluation
consistently for ALL comparable controllers;do not move candidate-specific
work outside the timing boundary to obtain a pass. Report actual workspace,
serialization and framework costs separately from online parameters.

5. CLOSEST METHODS AND NOVELTY BOUNDARY
BDQ(arXiv1711.08946),saved full extract lines336onward,already describes a
shared state-value estimator plus locally mean-centered branch advantages.
Its Eq1 is Q_d=V+A_d-mean(A_d). The present correction uses one summed joint
scalar and one joint scalar TD loss,with cost signs/scales and our mask;
BDQ uses branch values and its documented branch-learning/aggregation scheme.
The exact differences must be retained when adapting a BDQ baseline. They
are implementation/formulation differences,not proof of original priority.
Do not claim centering uniquely resolves all neural-parameter identifiability;
BDQ's own full text explicitly discusses limits of that interpretation.

DQfD(arXiv1704.03732),saved method extract lines330..405,combines demonstration
supervision with TD,n-step,regularization and replay treatment. Our BC stage
followed by ordinary TD is not full DQfD. That source already warns that
supervised-only pretraining does not make values Bellman-consistent.
Policy Distillation(arXiv1511.06295),saved Section3.2,already separates action
preferences from difficult Q-scale transfer. The known2025MEC DQfD-DECO work
(DOI10.1109/ICEAAI64185.2025.10956500)overlaps heuristic demonstration plus RL;
only abstract/metadata are available locally. Missing full methods remain an
access gap,not evidence that our extension is novel.

Thus this brief clears a concrete engineering correction question,not the
requested research contribution by itself. A later claim needs demonstrated
usefulness against these established alternatives and a precise additional
domain/method improvement;do not relabel this repair as a novel algorithm.

6. COMPARISON QUESTIONS BEFORE RESEARCH
The eventual comparison should distinguish the following,with exact budgets,
RNG blocks,optimizer settings and analysis decisions frozen prospectively:
A. corrected centered-linear BC->DDQN (engineering candidate).
B. current uncentered-softplus BC->DDQN,retrained on common fresh seeds.
C. centered-softplus BC->DDQN:Q=-softplus(-V+sum A_selected).
   Same heads/scale/BC;AversusC isolates removing the outer saturating transform
   within the centered parameterization. No assumption that centering alone
   prevents its nonlinear saturation.
D. uncentered-linear BC->DDQN:Q=V-sum z_selected.
   AversusD tests parameterization/optimization under the same linear scalar
   route,not added expressive capacity. These equations intentionally make
   V's sign convention explicit;do not silently negate initialized rows.
E. corrected centered-linear BC-only student,trained on the same demonstrations
   for the same BC budget,then frozen. Collect no further training transitions
   for this arm just to pretend data/compute are equal. Account for that gap.
   AversusE tests whether DDQN adds policy benefit beyond imitation;value
   accuracy is not expected from BC-only because its V row lacks direct labels.
F. corrected centered-linear DDQN with shared demo replay but no BC,plus
   ordinary corrected no-demo DDQN:separate initialization and data-access
   effects. Specify shared data/access and extra updates honestly.
G. frozen teacher and stronger compact/wide/DDQN/BDQ references under equal
   observation and fair implementation opportunities in the full comparison.

This is a comparison REQUIREMENTS MENU,not a released arm list or open-ended
permission to train eight variants. Before a new protocol,select the minimum
arm set that answers its stated claim,document deferred questions,and avoid
dropping stronger controls merely to pass. Any reduced pilot cannot answer
the omitted mechanism or contribution questions. Final-only checkpoints
remain the default;BC-only is a distinct declared arm,not a retrospectively
selected intermediate checkpoint of A. No best-fit selection.

7. ACCEPTANCE MEANING AND NEXT BOUNDED ENGINEERING GATE
The immediate gate is one isolated prototype for A with small equation-level
reference functions for C,D. Do not integrate a research runner or assign
development seeds in that gate. Verify:
- Exact parameter/storage counts,all-action mean centering,offset invariance,
  and analytic V/advantage derivatives against autograd on synthetic inputs.
- Masked greedy equality with chosen Q,and exhaustive reduced joint-action
  checks;mean centering includes all5actions even when local is excluded.
- Teacher-guided preferences,zero direct BC gradient to the V output row,
  shared-feature gradient behavior,and independent BC-only freeze semantics.
- Online selection/separate target evaluation,detached targets,no terminal
  decode,finite/sign REPORTING(no enforced nonpositivity),checkpoint reload,
  no hidden teacher in student deployment,and fail-closed research CLI.
- Compare scalar derivative behavior of old softplus and the new linear route
  on fixed synthetic scores;this proves the removed local attenuation factor,
  not superior trained stability or policy quality.
Use isolated fixture seeds,retain failures,perform no real task rollouts or
research optimizer budget. Small synthetic learning steps may test mechanics
if counted explicitly. Passing this gate leads to a prospective comparison
freeze/readiness stage,not automatic training or thesis claim revision.

Scientific criteria for any later research run must assess BOTH restoring
usable scalar TD learning AND policy behavior,including the BC-only contrast.
No new numerical calibration/utility threshold is chosen from old failures.
Report all fits,uncertainty,teacher restrictions,near-zero outputs,gradient
distributions,return/deadlines,storage and full inference cost. A successful
mechanics derivative check cannot substitute for those outcomes.

PRESERVATION
No implementation,simulation,optimizer updates,new research seeds,retiming
or Word-thesis edits in THIS design stage. Current pilot promising result
remains passed;all prior failed protocols remain failed. Final reservations
untouched. Security/trust deferred;cloud/charging excluded.
