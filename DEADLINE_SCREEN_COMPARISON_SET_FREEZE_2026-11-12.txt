WHY THESE CONTROLLERS
The unmasked primary differs only by disabling screening in greedy behavior,
exploration and online next-action selection. Network, value definition and
inherited horizon/anchor mechanisms remain identical. Paired randomness does
not mean identical realized decisions, replay contents or queue histories.

The screened no-price ablation retains identical tensors and inherited losses
while zeroing the price contribution. The screened plain24 anchored baseline
has the same hidden width and inherited losses; its parameter count and value
parameterization differ, so it is an architecture control, not a price ablation.

Plain small DDQN is included both screened and unmasked. It uses the existing
plain24 nonpositive scalar critic without horizon scaling or extra anchor loss.
It remains factorized: plain DDQN does not mean exponentially large joint-action
enumeration. Screened baselines receive exactly the primary local screen.

Screened compact64 inherits the primary horizon/anchor mechanisms. Unmasked
compact64 is the original nonpositive reference without those corrections.
This compact pair does not isolate screening. Wide128 screened/unmasked does
isolate screening; neither wide arm gains horizon scaling or anchor loss.
Screened adapted BDQ64 retains branchwise Huber and mean chosen branch value.
It is a disclosed adaptation, not an exact replication of the full BDQ paper.

FIXED HEURISTICS
nearest_rsu and uav_selective are unchanged strong policy references. all_local
is an unchanged diagnostic floor and is not an acceptance reference. These
heuristics receive no learned screen or fallback. All-local may knowingly
choose locally infeasible tasks. No arm uses future/private state information.

screened_price24 vs screened_plain24_anchored: Same-width architecture comparison; not one-factor ablation.

screened_wide128 vs unmasked_wide128: Screening effect within wide128.
screened_plain24_anchored vs screened_plain24: Combined horizon/anchor effect; does not separate mechanisms.
Report primary return/deadline outcomes against all ten other learned arms
and all three heuristics per initialization, irrespective of whether it wins.

INTERPRETATION
The focus remains a measured low-complexity DDQN implementation improvement.
Engineering success does not establish competitive policy performance or
calibration. If screened plain DDQN matches the primary, price superiority is
unsupported. Masking alone is not new. Broader UAV-IoV literature comparison
and supervisor discussion of the contribution remain outstanding.
