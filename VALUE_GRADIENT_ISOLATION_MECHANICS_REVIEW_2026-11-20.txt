WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
The proposal and the adapted dueling-gradient rescaling control are new
adapters of the preserved calibrated critic. The module also exposes the
seven inherited controls without mutating the original arm registry.
Every model retains the original parameter/buffer layout and deployment
forward. Only the explicit td_prediction method invokes a custom affine
backward for the two new arms:

P: readout weight/bias gradients are ordinary; the feature gradient excludes
   the last/V output row. Branch TD gradients remain active.
R: all TD feature-gradient paths remain, multiplied by 1/sqrt(2).
   Readout gradients are ordinary.

Retention uses its ordinary separate forward graph. Its hidden/branch
gradients are not rescaled or blocked. BC, calibration, greedy selection,
selected-Q evaluation and target forward use the preserved ordinary path.
There is no persistent routing mode that can leak into those operations.

The online step uses one detached DDQN target, one joint TD+retention
backward, global norm clipping at 10 and exactly one Adam step. It does not
copy target weights: the later runner must control the frozen target-copy
schedule. Parameters AND buffers are checked for online/target aliasing.

   gradients remain correct. R has its declared hidden rescaling.

   Calibration handoffs match across P/R/A and do not mutate their inputs.

10. Invalid objectives, alpha, masks, terminal flags, optimizer settings,
