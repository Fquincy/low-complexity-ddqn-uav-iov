IMPLEMENTATION
assignment_price_native_heads_runtime.py exports only the original neural
core, returning one raw FP32 vector. Native code fuses softplus/division,
softmax and existing workload/decoder finishing. FP32 head transforms and
workload products remain FP32; potential/decoder work remains FP64, fastmath
off. No equation, network, replay-value definition, sweep count, mask, stable
vehicle order, exact-tie rule, DDQN update or loss was changed. It calls the
same previously tested coordinate decoder and uses its selected full cost.
Queue preparation retains expm1, not exp-minus-one. All four arms supported.

Backend softplus/softmax and reduction rounding may differ from PyTorch.
Equivalence here means exact actions on tested inputs and the existing head/
value tolerances; it is not universal bitwise identity. No claim that all
possible near ties or future trained checkpoints will agree. Training stays
on the original differentiable critic; exported runtime is an immutable
snapshot and must be rebuilt and checked after training updates.
