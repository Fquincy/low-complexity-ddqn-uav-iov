HORIZON-NORMALIZED DDQN LOSS: PROSPECTIVE CORRECTION BRIEF
Date: November14,2026
Status: design/amendment only; no implementation, seed reservation or launch.

DECISION AND SCOPE
Test one training correction: normalize each uniform-replay Bellman residual
by that transition's remaining horizon BEFORE applying Huber. Retain the
screened compact price24 primary, its frozen neural architecture, exact decoder
and inherited terminal anchor. Give no-price24 and anchored plain24 the same
correction to distinguish general learning benefit from architecture benefit.
This does not invent a new remote feasibility rule or change reward weights.
It is a prospective hypothesis, not an explanation established by saved traces.

WHY THIS BOUNDED QUESTION
Screening removed impossible-local choices and all local misses in the saved
screened traces, yet remote CPU and upload failures remain. The primary's
Q-G bias is negative in all5fits, with large early-horizon error. No-price
also exhibits pessimism. The current Q=-h*c_theta parameterization scales its
training derivatives with remaining horizon h; ordinary Huber on Q-y therefore
has different per-state gradient scales across h. A normalization correction
can be tested without new weights, information or online action overhead.
The diagnosis did NOT prove gradient scaling caused policy/calibration failure.
The correction may underfit early-episode total return, fail remote choices,
or worsen deadline performance. Those outcomes must remain reportable failures.

1. UNCHANGED CRITIC, STATE AND ACTION SPACE
H=40, h(s)=40-round(40*s[-1]); h in1..40 for executed states.
For each horizon-scaled controller Q_theta(s,a)=-h(s)*c_theta(s,a), with its
original nonnegative cost parameterization and unchanged price/plain tables.
All533observed features remain. The controller receives no exact private
queue history, future arrivals, completed-delay labels or new feasibility oracle.
The conservative interval local screen is unchanged. All four remote choices
and all movement actions stay available. There is no remote deadline guarantee.
Q=0 at h=0; terminal observations are never decoded.

2. MASK-AWARE DOUBLE DQN TARGET
For each nonterminal transition, choose
  a_next = argmax_(a in existing allowed set(s_next)) Q_online(s_next,a).
Evaluate exactly that chosen action with the target network:
  y = r + Q_target(s_next,a_next), gamma=1.
For terminal transitions y=r, with no next-state action decoding.
Target values are detached; target-copy schedule and behavior policy unchanged.
Do not substitute current-state masks or let the target select a second action.

3. THE ONLY NEW TRAINING MECHANISM
Old uniform loss per transition:
  ell_old = Huber_1(Q_theta(s,a)-stopgrad(y)).
New uniform loss per transition:
  z_theta = Q_theta(s,a)/h(s)
  z_target = stopgrad(y)/h(s)
  ell_new = Huber_1(z_theta-z_target).
Use current-state h, NOT next-state h, and divide BOTH operands before Huber.
Do not divide the scalar old loss by h: Huber(e/h) != Huber(e)/h.
The Huber threshold1 is now in reward-per-remaining-step units. This changes
both residual scaling and the clipping regime; neither effect is hidden.
No adaptive normalizer, learned statistics, clipping of y, new reward scale,
or normalization fitted to evaluation outcomes.

For the anchored arms, retain the exact separate terminal-anchor loss:
  L = mean_uniform(ell_new) + 1.0*mean_terminal Huber_1(Q(s,a)-r).
Anchor samples have current h=1, so the explicit formula there is unchanged.
Uniform batch128 and anchor batch32 retain their existing sampling rules,
empty-pool handling, optimizer step count and separate RNG. The correction
changes relative uniform/terminal influence through its defined scaling;
it is not claimed to isolate gradient magnitude from every loss-weight effect.

Equivalent average-cost recurrence for c=-Q/h at h>1:
  c_target = (-r + (h-1)*c_target_network(s_next,a_next))/h.
At h=1, c_target=-r. This is an algebraic rewriting of gamma1 finite-horizon
DDQN, not a new objective, proof of convergence, or guarantee of calibration.
For Q=-h*c, d ell_new/d theta = -psi_1((Q-y)/h)*d c/d theta,
whereas d ell_old/d theta = -h*psi_1(Q-y)*d c/d theta.
This motivates the experiment; identical zero-residual fixed points do not
imply identical projected solutions with finite capacity/noisy replay.

4. REMOTE DECISIONS: REQUIRED OUTCOME, NO ASSUMED FIX
Fit0's recorded RSU1 congestion and fit3's UAV1 upload failures are distinct.
The unchanged architecture has workload/radio observations, but screening
alone does not certify remote choices. This correction asks whether changed
value learning improves those decisions. Remote outcome diagnosis is a
required report: deadlines/misses by destination, upload/CPU stage and time,
loads, action frequencies and movement. Preserve all five initializations.
Do not declare success merely because Q calibration improves. If remote
performance remains inadequate, this candidate fails the combined decision.
A queue/link decoder, new remote mask, rollout planner, reward/penalty change,
additional head or architecture simplification is outside this brief.

5. EXPECTED COMPLEXITY AND LIMITS
Zero added model parameters or buffers. Primary remains17,066parameters,
68,264FP32 parameter bytes +20buffer bytes; no-price same; plain24
16,841parameters/67,364bytes +20buffer bytes. Verify live implementation.
Action selection and the deployed forward/decoder are unchanged. There are
O(B) extra scalar operations for B uniform replay samples, no additional
network forward or target evaluations. Horizon computation is existing code.
Actual trained-checkpoint inference equivalence/storage/latency must still
be checked; unchanged architecture is not a substitute for measurements.
This correction does not make the primary smaller than plain24. It aims at
better performance per measured cost versus both compact and wide controls.

6. PROSPECTIVE COMPARISON AMENDMENT: NO WINNER SUBSTITUTION
Retain all11 learner definitions and3heuristics from the completed November12
protocol as fresh-run controls. Add exactly3named normalized-loss arms:
  normalized_screened_price24 (new primary),
  normalized_screened_no_price24,
  normalized_screened_plain24_anchored.
Thus14learned arms plus3heuristics if this design proceeds to research.
Every control reruns on the SAME fresh paired initialization/workload blocks;
old consumed runs are historical evidence, not matched fresh controls.
Ordinary small DDQN, wide128, adapted BDQ and their declared original losses
remain untouched. BDQ never silently gets a scalar branch-mean objective.
There is no choosing whichever of price/no-price/plain24 wins as the primary.

Exact correction ablations: normalized vs original loss within each of the
three anchored architectures. Primary must show positive correction evidence
against screened_price24, not only improve on weak references. Price benefit
must be tested against normalized_screened_no_price24. Same-width benefit
must be tested against normalized_screened_plain24_anchored. Report all three
correction effects regardless of direction. Previous screening evidence is
preserved; no second claim that normalization itself established masking benefit.

Retain existing per-fit reference margins (return>=-2,deadline>=-1point),
strong heuristic nondegradation, primary value limits and cost ceilings.
Require nondegradation in both policy metrics per fit and equal-fit mean
return gain>=2/deadline gain>=1point for each new primary mechanism claim
(correction, price, same-width architecture). The new primary must also pass
all five-fit global value screens in ORIGINAL Q/return units:
RMSEnormalized<=.25,bias<=2,nearzero-large-return fraction<=.05,
terminal abs-error p95<=2; nonpositive/finite values and diagnostics.
Normalization of the training loss does not normalize away evaluation error.
Report distributions of both original-unit TD residuals and normalized
residuals by horizon. Reduced normalized loss alone is not a success claim.

7. FAIRNESS, FRESHNESS AND AUTHORIZATION
Before research reserve/collision-audit new disjoint model/task/RNG blocks,
predeclare fixed budgets and all14-arm arithmetic, optimizer settings,
checkpoints, timing, descriptive uncertainty and independent audit workflow.
No budget or fresh seed is declared or consumed by this brief. Do not quietly
inherit440,000slots: more arms require a separately explicit budget decision.
Final historical seed sets remain protected. Retain all failed completed runs.
No evaluation-based tuning, favorable retiming, extra fits or best checkpoint.

8. ENGINEERING CHECKS BEFORE A RESEARCH RUN
Implement the loss in a new versioned adapter; preserve frozen old sources.
Check current h vs next h, terminal bypass, detached targets, correct division
inside Huber, unchanged terminal anchor, branch-specific losses and exact
one-step gradients on controlled scalar cases. Confirm coefficients and
gradient clipping follow the declared formula, not old-unit clipping logic.
Check unchanged zero-residual targets and original-unit diagnostics.
Then use an isolated synthetic fixture for paired RNG/replay, final-only
checkpoint freezes, exact eager/runtime actions and independent summaries.
Use the corrected batch-one auditor for saved evaluation predictions and
stream logs/compact summaries. Synthetic outcomes cannot tune the design.
Research launch remains a later explicit decision, not part of this brief.

9. NOVELTY AND STOPPING BOUNDARY
Horizon normalization, Huber loss, Double DQN, action masks and branching
critics are established ideas. This brief supplies no new algorithmic novelty
proof. A defensible contribution would need successful measured controller
improvement within UAV-IoV, equally informed controls, positive ablations,
independent final evidence, relevant literature comparison and supervisor
agreement. Do not label a routine loss normalization novel by itself.
If normalized plain24 matches/beats the primary or price benefit fails,
price-architecture superiority remains unsupported. If calibration improves
but deadline/return fails, the complete contribution remains unestablished.
Do not retrospectively replace thresholds or promote the best comparator.

SCOPE

charging stations or security/trust in this design. Word thesis unchanged.
The next authorized engineering stage would implement/test this exact
candidate; it would not launch research training or final evaluation.
