teacher corrections or reward changes. Preserve unfinished tasks and original

in EVERY initialization. G_own is within-episode undiscounted realized return;

Own-policy errors compare different trajectories; no causal calibration claim.

full artifact audit. Equality uses recorded values,without scientific eps.
