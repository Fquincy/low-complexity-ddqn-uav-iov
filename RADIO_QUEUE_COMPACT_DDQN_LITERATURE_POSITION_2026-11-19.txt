CURRENTLY VERIFIED OVERLAP
1. Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461
   Live abstract: online selection/target evaluation and reducing overestimation
   are established. Retaining DDQN is not our new contribution.
2. Action Branching Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08946
   Live abstract: shared decision layers, per-action branches and linear output
   growth already exist. Small branching DDQN alone is insufficient novelty.
3. Deep Coordination Graphs.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.00091
   Live abstract: pairwise factorized learned payoffs, message passing, parameter
   sharing and low-rank approximation are existing mechanisms. A sparse pair
   potential is not new simply because it is used in a DDQN architecture.
4. Optimizing vehicular edge computing: graph-based double-DQN approaches
   for intelligent task offloading.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s11227-024-06599-4
   Crossref verifies publication metadata. The associated preprint record
   https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-4917636/v1 includes an abstract describing
   GCN+Double-DQN for offloading. Do not assume journal and preprint methods
   are identical without their full texts. Graph-based DDQN in VEC exists.
5. Joint Task Offloading and Resource Optimization in NOMA-based Vehicular
   Edge Computing: A Game-Theoretic DRL Approach.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.12749
   Live abstract includes interference and stringent deadlines. Its NOMA
   channel differs from ours; radio/deadline-aware DRL is still established.
6. Intelligent Offloading in Vehicular Edge Computing: A Comprehensive
   Review of Deep Reinforcement Learning Approaches and Architectures.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.06963
   Live abstract identifies a survey of DRL offloading; use its references
   to widen the review, not as primary proof of a particular algorithm.

NARROW HYPOTHESIS TO CHECK AGAINST FULL METHODS
One 24-unit DDQN critic combining correctable signed residuals with a
deadline-weighted two-signal radio interaction proxy, deterministic K4 donor
sparsification and cached single-pass exact surrogate coordinate updates.
The intended gain is measured policy/cost tradeoff in the existing UAV-IoV
problem, not invention of graphs, residual learning, interference equations,
coordinate descent, masking or Double DQN.

FULL-TEXT QUESTIONS BEFORE CLAIMING NOVELTY
- Does an existing offloading controller use the same pair interference
  marginal, deadline-slack weighting and sparse donor selection?
- Does prior coordination-graph RL already provide the same bounded decoder
  or a cheaper/better equivalent? Compare equations and operation counts.
- What do graph-DDQN offloading methods learn: topology embeddings, independent
  branch values, pair payoffs or physical residual costs? Avoid title-based
  claims of difference. Extract exact state/actions, cost, decoder and losses.
- What is lost by sparse current-position pairs versus joint upload histories
  and coupled UAV movement? Can the proposed comparison isolate that loss?
- Does the advantage survive plain-small, fixed-physics, observed-load/radio,
  incumbent and pair-removal controls on matched fresh workloads?
