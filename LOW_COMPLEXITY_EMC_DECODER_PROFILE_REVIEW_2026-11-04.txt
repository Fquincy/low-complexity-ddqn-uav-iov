EMC-Q EXACT DECODER PROFILING REVIEW
Date: November 4, 2026
Status: profiling and equivalent decoder checks complete; no fitting.

RESULT
Changing the exact contraction implementation gives only a modest local
full-action median difference. No implementation measured here is faster
than either additive control. A lower-latency claim remains unsupported.
The original emc_q.py and all existing model/simulator sources were preserved.
New alternatives are isolated in emc_decoder_variants.py.

WHAT WAS CHECKED
Two equivalent decoders: batched matrix multiplication and explicit rank2
broadcasting, versus the original einsum contraction. The model, output
representation, movement enumeration, reward and action layout were unchanged.
All checked actions matched exactly. Maximum Q-value difference was
4.77e-7 due to floating-point evaluation order. Checks covered60 reduced
batched random cases, native model batches1/7/128, all-action ties and an
explicit movement/destination coupling example. They do not guarantee the
same tie resolution for every untested near-tied float32 input.
The original backward/target tests remain relevant because chosen-action
training and target detachment were not modified. No optimizer update or
simulator interaction was performed.

MATCHED COMPLETE-ACTION TIMING
One Torch intra-op/inter-op thread, CPU, untrained model. Same128 recorded
observations for all paths, five randomized block rounds,32 warmups and128

correctness assertions excluded. No model weights changed. Every timed EMC

ISOLATED COMPONENT TIMING
One fixed observation, cached upstream values;256 calls/component, after32
warmups. These component medians are NOT additive and cannot be subtracted
from full-action medians to compute exact time fractions. Separate component
blocks can experience different scheduling, frequency or runtime conditions.
decoder_only_matmul: median 0.44320ms, p95 0.64005ms.
decoder_only_original_einsum: median 0.27030ms, p95 0.64665ms.
decoder_only_rank2_broadcast: median 0.26120ms, p95 0.34275ms.
encoder_only: median 0.04795ms, p95 0.05470ms.
head_only: median 0.01820ms, p95 0.01937ms.
The decoder involves several small operations and reductions, whereas the
encoder/head alone are relatively short in these blocks. However, isolated
matmul timing is slower than isolated original even though full-action matmul
median is lower. This inconsistency prevents a robust isolated-kernel speedup
claim. Hardware power/thermal state and OS scheduling were not controlled.
Only this same-run benchmark is reported; older absolute timings are not a
paired speedup comparison. Torch compilation/native deployment was not tested.

COST CLAIM AND STOPPING
EMC still has69361 parameters/277444 float32 parameter bytes:34.3% fewer
than additive128, but42.3% more than compact additive64. Parameter storage is
not peak memory, inference energy or training computation. Quality remains
untested. A long learning pilot is not authorized by a timing microbenchmark.
Do not adopt matmul as a proven faster default from one noisy benchmark. The
variants are available for a separately predeclared runner; original remains
the correctness reference.

RECOMMENDED NEXT STEP
Freeze a bounded storage-versus-quality experiment specification before any
training: EMC versus original128x128 and compact64x64 additive controls,
disjoint training seed blocks, paired action/replay streams, detached target
graphs, final episode metrics and common fresh greedy evaluation. Explicitly
state that the candidate has a latency penalty; define a maximum acceptable
latency cost and quality noninferiority/improvement margins BEFORE results.
Against compact additive, interaction benefits would need to justify both
additional parameter storage and execution cost. No novelty claim follows
from the restricted movement-conditioning mechanism.
If lower latency than additive is mandatory, stop here and redesign the
representation/decoder instead of training this version. If lower storage
than the original baseline is the intended claim, test quality under that
narrow claim; do not rename it lower latency or lower total complexity.
