RETURN-CALIBRATED HANDOFF: DEVELOPMENT RESULTS REVIEW
Date: 2026-11-20

DECISION
The frozen 51,280-slot comparison is complete, audited and independently
reverified against its D: archive. The value screen PASSED; the policy
screen FAILED. The frozen combined decision is promising=false. Preserve
every fit and comparator. Hold expansion and final evaluation. This is a
measured improvement over the uncalibrated correction, but it does not yet
establish the proposed low-complexity DDQN contribution.

NEXT BOUNDED GATE
Diagnose saved data before proposing another change. Compare A against E
and F on paired workloads, concentrating on fit 2 and the observed deadline
destinations; separate action-ranking changes from common value shifts and
inspect saved Bellman targets, TD/retention losses, gradient groups and
terminal/nonterminal errors. Include B/C/D to examine component interactions.
Use fixed saved states for checkpoint comparisons; trajectories diverge,
so raw trajectory differences alone do not establish a causal mechanism.
Specify what the saved data can and cannot resolve without new rollouts.

Do not retrain, select a best fit, promote E as the proposed DDQN result,
alter historical thresholds, or start final evaluation. A prospective
design/comparison decision follows diagnosis only. Stronger compact64/
wide128, adapted BDQ/full DQfD, novelty assessment and untouched final
evaluation remain requirements before a broad thesis efficiency claim.
Related imitation/DQfD/distillation/kickstarting work prevents treating
the mere combination of familiar mechanisms as established novelty.

Contribution one remains efficient DDQN offloading in UAV-IoV. Security/
trust is deferred; cloud and charging remain excluded. No new research
launch is authorized by this review. The Word thesis remains unchanged.
