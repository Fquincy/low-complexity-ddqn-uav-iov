A Closer Look at Invalid Action Masking in Policy Gradient Algorithms
https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14171
Invalid action masking is established; this paper concerns policy-gradient algorithms. Its abstract does not prove masked-DDQN convergence or deadline guarantees.

Action Branching Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning
https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.08946
Shared branching architectures with linear output growth and BDQ are established. Linear decoder scaling alone is not a new contribution.

Deep Reinforcement Learning with Double Q-learning
https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.06461
Separating online action selection and target evaluation is established Double DQN. It does not guarantee calibration in this simulator.

Constrained Policy Optimization
https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.10528
Reward-plus-constraint formulations are established. This proposal is not CPO and inherits no CPO constraint-satisfaction guarantee.
