OUTCOME

No IoV simulator episodes, new research training or final-seed evaluation

DESIGN REVIEW AND FROZEN ENGINEERING RULES
Protocol: TERMINAL_ANCHOR_ENGINEERING_PROTOCOL_2026-11-06.json.
Use Q(s,a)=h(s)*Qbase(s,a), where h=40-round(40*normalized_time).
Validate time lies on the discrete slot grid; terminal value is exactly zero.
For positive h use the unchanged base decoder. Reject terminal decoding.
Keep the DDQN online-selection/target-evaluation update and skip terminal
next-state decoding. Train with the uniform DDQN Huber loss plus a separate
mean terminal Huber loss of weight1, using32 terminal samples with replacement.
An empty terminal pool contributes zero anchor loss and is explicitly logged.
Use an independent terminal-sampling RNG and remove evicted terminal indices.
No extra optimizer steps are introduced. Training cost increases because of
the additional terminal loss; inference parameter storage is unchanged.
