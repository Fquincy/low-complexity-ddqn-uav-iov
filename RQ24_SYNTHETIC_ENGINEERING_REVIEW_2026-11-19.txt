COMPLEXITY QUALIFICATION
The design brief's local-update expression O(ND+E) omits sorting tasks by
cycles. This implementation uses stable mergesort: total decoder includes
O(N log N+ND+E), plus forward/features. Sparse donor selection costs O(UNK)
with bounded insertion arrays; K4 is fixed. An optimized heap could provide
the brief's logK factor, but no heap or strictly-linear whole-controller
claim is implemented. Undirected degree may exceedK, yet summing all
incident edge visits is O(E). No dense N-by-N edge table is allocated.
This accounting correction does not change the declared architecture or
rescue the failed measured gate.
