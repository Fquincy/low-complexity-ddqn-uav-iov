365 updates, and passed the read-only artifact audit. The audit checked the
five checkpoint/source snapshots, paired training and fresh workloads,
bounded greedy-value signs, optimizer rows, timing records and evaluation

constraint; it is not evidence of comparative learning quality.

and bounded EMC-Q's fresh-terminal absolute-error p95 was 24.51, above the
development calibration screen of 10. Because smoke outcomes are explicitly
integration-only, these observations must not be used to change thresholds,
seeds, rewards, optimizer settings or model selection.

NEXT DECISION
The frozen 120,000-slot experiment completed on November 4, 2026. It used the
predeclared three task-seed blocks and model seeds, trained all 15 models for
8,000 slots each, and completed 104,655 optimizer updates. All 15 checkpoints
were frozen before fresh evaluation. The independent saved-artifact audit
passed, checking 3,000 training episodes, 375 validation episodes, 720 model
fresh episodes, 144 heuristic fresh episodes, 8 probes, 15 checkpoints,
6,194 manifest entries and every optimizer row. The auditor added zero
simulator steps and zero optimizer updates.

The predeclared aggregate quality gate FAILED because all three primary
initializations had to pass, but initialization 3119 failed all five policy
comparisons. Initialization 3109 passed all policy comparisons and calibration
but failed the median latency ratio: 0.550 ms versus additive128 at 0.1098 ms,
ratio 5.009 against the 5.0 limit. Initialization 3121 passed every screen.
All three passed the value sign and terminal calibration screens. The result
is therefore mixed across model seeds and is not a consistent quality win.

The conclusion remains a development engineering result, not formal
noninferiority, statistical significance, novelty, deployment performance or
a proven thesis contribution. No protocol settings were tuned from these
results. Do not select only the passing initialization or silently rerun.
The next step is to document and discuss the failed gate, then predeclare any
new experiment before using new seeds. The Word thesis remains unchanged.

This recommendation is about implementation risk, not the bounded arms'
two-episode smoke performance. Those smoke outcomes are explicitly excluded
from tuning or model selection. After the reduced end-to-end fixture passes,
the existing CLI confirmation can be used for a separate launch decision.
Do not change frozen rewards, seeds, thresholds, optimizer settings or model
definitions based on the smoke. The Word thesis remains unchanged.
