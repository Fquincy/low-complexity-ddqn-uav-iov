NONPOSITIVE-Q VALUE-BOUND PAIRED SMOKE REVIEW
Date: November 4, 2026
Scope: Frozen five-arm integration smoke and read-only artifact audit.

DECISION
The bounded-model implementation, focused tests, paired runner and reserved
smoke passed the integration gate. This does not establish policy quality,
low-complexity superiority, novelty, statistical significance or deployment
performance. No development run was launched. The Word thesis was not edited.

IMPLEMENTATION
The separate runner is run_value_bound_tradeoff.py. Its five arms are the
frozen additive128 unbounded, additive64 unbounded, EMC-Q unbounded,
additive64 nonpositive and EMC-Q nonpositive variants. For bounded arms,
Q_b(s,a) = -softplus(Q_raw(s,a)); the existing architecture parameter counts
are unchanged. Additive action selection minimizes each raw head. Bounded EMC-Q
negates its unary, movement and rank-2 factor tables and uses the existing exact
max decoder, which produces the exact raw-score minimum. Double-DQN target
evaluation applies the same transform at the online-selected action, under
no-grad, and terminal successors are skipped.

Eight focused tests passed. They checked the transform's sign and monotonicity,
additive raw-argmin selection, exhaustive small-space EMC decoder equivalence,
Double-DQN target selection and terminal handling, all five model/checkpoint
round-trips, paired exploration and update counters, a paired physical
episode, and that this runner refuses development mode.

SMOKE AND AUDIT
The reserved smoke used model seed 3091 and its frozen disjoint task seeds.
Each of five arms trained for 80 slots, 73 optimizer updates and 9 target
copies: 400 training slots and 365 updates total. Validation, fixed probes,
fresh episodes and timing ran as predeclared; no development training followed.

The read-only audit passed. It checked all five checkpoint hashes and source
snapshots, 365 optimizer rows, paired training and fresh workloads, bounded
greedy-value signs, replay and saved trajectory shapes, 30 evaluation-state
invariance checks, and the run manifest. The audit itself performed zero
simulator steps and zero optimizer updates.

Two fresh smoke episodes per arm produced these descriptive averages:

These tiny-sample outcomes are not suitable for ranking, tuning or selecting
an architecture. The bounded EMC-Q fresh-terminal absolute-error p95 was
24.51 reward units, above the frozen development calibration screen of 10;
the smoke does not pass or fail that development screen. It only shows that
the constrained path runs and enforces the known sign bound. The bounded
EMC-Q measured median action time was 0.545 ms on this local smoke protocol;
this is diagnostic, not a device-independent latency claim.
