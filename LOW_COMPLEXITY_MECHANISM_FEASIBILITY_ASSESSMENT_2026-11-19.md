# Existing-controller mechanism and feasibility assessment

Date: 2026-11-19. **Decision: HOLD new candidate selection and research launch.**

The concrete question is whether an **existing** mechanism supplies both a defensible improvement over an equally informed small DDQN and a credible complete-runtime path. None currently satisfies both conditions. This does not prove that efficient DDQN is impossible. It means the evidence does not justify automatically spending another training budget on the examined designs.

## What the closest method texts actually establish

The following passages were checked in existing local full-text extracts during this assessment. This is targeted method verification, not a new systematic literature search or publication-priority clearance. Extract line numbers identify local text, not publication page numbers.

- **BDQ, arXiv:1711.08946:** `branching_full.txt`, lines 203-211 and 336, describes shared decision features, branchwise action values and a common value estimator. Line 292 describes online action selection with separate target-network evaluation. Consequently neither shared small branches nor the DDQN update alone distinguishes this project.
- **Masked UAV offloading, arXiv:2407.19657:** `uav_masking.txt`, lines 667-679, defines a state-dependent mask and describes battery/computational feasibility. Our observation-interval local-service predicate differs from that documented mask description. These passages do not prove that conservative interval screening is absent throughout prior literature, nor that the difference is a sufficient research contribution.
- **Vehicular DDQN, arXiv:2404.07215:** `vehicular_ddqn.txt`, line 155, describes terminal selection using mobility/server load and server-side DDQN request scheduling. Line 484 explicitly discusses simultaneous terminal choices, congestion and inaccurate revenue evaluation. ADP's joint assignment potential differs from that described two-stage scheduler, but congestion awareness alone is already established.
- **Hierarchical UAV-IoV, arXiv:2507.05722:** `hierarchical_iov.txt`, lines 807-821, uses reachable links, remaining compute above 30% and a distance/resource score. This is direct overlap with learned control plus resource-informed deterministic allocation. Merely combining a network and allocator is insufficient distinction.

Prior saved sources additionally document FlexEdge's execution-complexity discussion and coordination-graph overlap. They are context, not newly reproduced equations in this assessment. The known unavailable/metadata-only papers remain gaps; missing text is not evidence that an idea is new.

## Why the two most plausible existing routes are not released

**Screened original-loss price24:** the physical predicate is cheap, transparent and supported by a useful ablation in one completed study. However the basic necessary condition `cycles/CPU_rate > deadline` is elementary; outward rounding protects against quantization error but has not been shown to provide a research-level distinction. Giving the same screen to plain DDQN removes the basis for crediting all screening gains to the price architecture. Existing same-width and no-price outcomes are precisely why those controls cannot be omitted. Retain it as an informative reference, not an exploratory winner promoted to confirmed primary.

**ADP:** its actual-assignment insertion marginal is a concrete equation-level change from the separable price table. That makes it testable, not automatically useful or new. The completed test already found inconsistent mechanism benefit and a stronger no-learning comparator. The offline diagnostics located gradient conflicts and link/deadline failures but did not prove that one cheap correction resolves them. Removing required decoder work or changing its physical potential would be a new design; speculative savings are insufficient for launch.

Thus the strongest policy-component evidence and the most specific mechanism hypothesis belong to different pieces of the project. Combining their best historical properties into one claimed successful controller would be invalid.

## Recommendation and stopping point

1. Preserve the screened original-loss controller as a **development reference**, ADP as a completed failed primary, and RQ24/AR24 as parked engineering candidates. No architecture is selected for new research training.
2. Preserve the original low-complexity objective as unmet. A prospective storage-policy-latency tradeoff remains a possible research scope, not an accepted or demonstrated contribution. No new margins, seeds, model definitions or calibration exemptions are frozen here.
3. End the automatic chain of implementation gates at this decision. Another generic correctness fixture, unchanged training run, favorable retiming or document-only rephrasing will not supply the missing mechanism evidence.

**What would reopen technical work:** one prospective controller-improvement brief must specify an actual change, its equation-level difference from the closest methods, why that change addresses an observed limitation, a matched control, and a credible complete-cost argument. It must openly state which original requirements it retains and independently justify any proposed claim change. A new expensive physical decoder without a supported cost route does not meet this requirement. Only then would implementation and a separately authorized fresh experiment be justified.

This assessment does not recommend a particular new mechanism merely to keep producing implementations. None of the existing assessed candidates currently earns a fresh-run recommendation. If the original combined goal is retained, substantive new design reasoning is needed; if a narrower tradeoff is chosen, novelty, fair comparisons and fresh evidence are still needed. Departmental acceptance is separate and cannot be guaranteed. Absence of supervisor support is not treated as a technical prohibition on future authorized work.
