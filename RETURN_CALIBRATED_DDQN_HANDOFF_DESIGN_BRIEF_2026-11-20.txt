PROSPECTIVE DESIGN: RETURN-CALIBRATED, POLICY-RETAINING DDQN HANDOFF
20 November 2026
DESIGN BRIEF ONLY - NO IMPLEMENTED CONTROLLER OR RESEARCH LAUNCH

1. DECISION AND SCIENTIFIC PURPOSE
Investigate ONE training-stage correction to the existing compact additive
DDQN. Keep its observation, architecture, feasibility screen and decoder.
After BC, reduce the arbitrary action-gap scale without changing action
rankings, then fit the state-value output to recorded demonstration returns.
During DDQN, retain an auxiliary imitation loss on fixed demonstrations.
Use factorial controls to separate calibration from imitation retention.
This is a prospective hypothesis, not a finding that the correction works.

The diagnosed study remains FAILED under both frozen screens. Preserve all
failed fits and archives. The earlier promising heuristic-initialization pilot
remains exploratory evidence with unresolved calibration/novelty limitations.
No old reward, seed, model, optimizer, threshold or result is changed.

A useful outcome would be an implemented training mechanism that improves
a same-size compact DDQN and adds value beyond stopping after imitation,
while retaining small deployment storage. Proving that outcome would still
require stronger reference comparisons and untouched final evaluation.
This brief cannot establish a new lightweight/low-complexity contribution.

2. OBSERVATIONS MOTIVATING THE CHANGE
Source: CENTERED_LINEAR_SAVED_TRACE_DIAGNOSIS_2026-11-20.txt and its bound
saved-data evidence. BC produced greedy Q means +43.229,+33.359,+39.155
on fixed demonstration states. After DDQN: +89.406,+72.141,+91.555.
At the first TD update, the same-state greedy-minus-sampled Q gap averaged
15.077,10.650,16.757; negative rewards could not offset it. Terminal masking
was correct. All observed primary evaluation misses occurred at RSUs after
substantial vehicle-ranking changes. The uncentered linear control had bad
value scale but much better policies: value inflation alone is not a proven
complete cause. No claim that a constant value offset repairs action ranking.

3. PRECISE DEPLOYMENT MODEL: UNCHANGED COMPACT FUNCTION CLASS
Retain 533 -> 24 ReLU -> 161 FP32 outputs: 150 placement scores,10 movement
scores and one signed state-value output V. Divide placement scores by30
and movement scores by2 as before. Let z_b,d denote those scaled scores,
for32branches with5actions each. Define
  C_theta(s,a) = sum_b [z_theta,b,a_b(s) - mean_(d=0..4) z_theta,b,d(s)]
  Q_theta(s,a) = V_theta(s) - C_theta(s,a).
For nonterminal states, decode the exact masked maximum of THIS critic:
  a_b* = argmin_(d in allowed_b(s)) z_theta,b,d(s).
Retain inherited conservative local feasibility masking and first-index ties.
No new RSU/queue/battery filter, physical lookahead, teacher call or destination
repair is added. The additive model does not solve the coupled physical
optimization exactly. Terminal next states bypass decoding and bootstrap zero.

4. TRAINING STAGE A: SHARED DEMONSTRATIONS AND BC
Proposed exposure:10teacher episodes/fit,40slots/episode, same400transitions
and action labels for every demonstration-using arm of that fit. BC uses
200updates,batch64,Adam0.001,temperature1, and the inherited branchwise CE
(mean vehicle CE plus mean movement CE), exactly as in the previous study.
BC draws and raw network initialization are paired across relevant arms.
Only the frozen teacher's saved actions/rewards are used; no teacher network
or hidden state is supplied to deployed students. Report demonstrations'
simulation and computation cost separately from student online cost.

Record AFTER_BC hashes, fixed-state probes and final BC-only checkpoints.
The prospective BC optimizer is discarded before DDQN; no optimizer-state
transfer is allowed. Do not use consumed prior research weights or traces
as training data. Existing evidence only motivates this prospective design.

5. TRAINING STAGE B: POSITIVE ACTION-GAP RESCALING
Use the first8demonstration episodes (320states) for calibration fitting.
The last2episodes (80states) withhold return targets for calibration checks;
their action labels ARE used by BC and retention. They are therefore NOT
unseen-policy/generalization data. Do not refit or select settings on them.

On the frozen AFTER_BC fitting states define
  S_r = max(1e-6, median_n |r_n|)
  R_max = max_n sum_b [max_d z_b,d(s_n) - min_d z_b,d(s_n)]
  alpha = S_r / (S_r + R_max).
Use ALL5actions for these ranges, including locally screened alternatives.
For R_max=0 set alpha=1 (the formula agrees when S_r>0).
Multiply the final-layer weights AND biases of the160branch outputs by
alpha; leave the hidden layer and V row unchanged at this substage.
This folds alpha into existing parameters; no deployment alpha lookup.

In exact arithmetic alpha>0 preserves every branch ranking and tie, and
rescales every same-state modeled action gap by alpha. On the fitting
states only, the total gap between any two modeled legal joint actions
is at most alpha*R_max <= S_r. This is not a bound on true regret or on
states outside that finite dataset. It does not constrain later TD learning.
Assert FP32 greedy actions unchanged on all400demo states; if rounding
changes an action, HOLD engineering instead of changing tie rules/alpha.
Report alpha, masks, margins, zero/underflow checks and range distributions.

6. TRAINING STAGE C: VALUE-ROW CALIBRATION WITH BRANCH POLICY FIXED
Compute every demonstration's undiscounted reward-to-go from its saved
40slot trajectory:
  G_t = sum_(k=t..39) r_k.
These are realized teacher-continuation targets. They are not optimal values
or returns of the current student's different policy; do not equate them.
Fit Q(s,a_teacher) to G while freezing the hidden layer and scaled branches.
Because Q=V-C, the V regression target is
  t_n = G_n + C_scaled(s_n,a_teacher,n).

Use only the first8episodes' return targets for fitting. Let h_n be the
24ReLU features, mu their column means, and
  sigma_j = max(1e-6, sqrt(mean_n (h_n,j-mu_j)^2))
  H_n,j = (h_n,j-mu_j)/sigma_j
  S_G = max(1e-6, sqrt(mean_n G_n^2))
  u_n = t_n/S_G; u_bar = mean_n u_n; rho=1e-3.
Deterministic FP64 ridge solve:
  beta = solve(H^T H/N + rho*I, H^T(u-u_bar)/N)
  w_V,j = S_G*beta_j/sigma_j
  b_V = S_G*u_bar - sum_j w_V,j*mu_j.
Store the fitted V row/bias as FP32. Keep all other weights unchanged.
The regression objective is mean squared normalized error plus rho||beta||^2;
the intercept is unpenalized. It has a unique slope solve for rho>0.
Do not claim exact interpolation, a pointwise nonpositivity bound or optimal
calibration. Report residual distributions on fitting AND withheld targets,
rank/conditioning, finite coefficients and float32 export discrepancies.
Bad withheld return fitting is a design failure to report, not permission
to adjust rho or repeat the fit with more episodes from the held-out set.

Fitting V alone preserves the deployed greedy policy exactly. Both scaling
and calibration preserve the same action decisions before DDQN in real
arithmetic; verify against the independently trained BC-only checkpoint.
At a fitting demo state, if the fitted demo Q has residual e_n,
  Q(s,a_greedy) = G_n + e_n + modeled_gap(greedy,teacher),
where that modeled gap is nonnegative and bounded by S_r for that dataset.
This describes a controlled initial scale, not a convergence theorem.

7. TRAINING STAGE D: DDQN WITH DEMONSTRATION RETENTION
After the handoff, ALL online parameters are trainable. Copy the online
weights into a detached target and start a fresh Adam optimizer. For online
replay transitions, keep gamma=1 and terminal masking:
  a_next = decode_theta(s_next)
  y = r + (1-done)*Q_target(s_next,a_next)
  L_TD = mean Huber_beta1(Q_theta(s,a)-stopgrad(y)).
Terminal rows bypass decoder/target computation. No reward scaling/clipping,
terminal anchor, new replay priority or multistep return target is added.

At each TD update draw one additional batch64 of demonstration states and
teacher actions with replacement using its own reserved sampler. Define
  L_keep = mean_vehicle CE(-z_theta,b/T, a_teacher,b)
         + mean_movement CE(-z_theta,b/T, a_teacher,b)
with inherited masks, and T=alpha for calibrated arms; T=1 otherwise.
Use the SAME temperature for BOTH vehicle and movement groups, after their
existing /30 and /2 scaling. Thus the calibrated handoff initially has the
same imitation probabilities as its unscaled AFTER_BC model; alpha does not
silently make the retention target uniform. T is a fixed training metadata
scalar, not adapted from outcomes. There is no extra teacher inference.

Primary update:
  L = L_TD + lambda_keep*L_keep; lambda_keep=1.
One backward pass, global gradient clip10, ONE Adam step per TD batch.
No separate gradient projection or freezing between these two losses.
There is no guarantee that this fixed loss weight preserves a policy;
CE gradients can be large when alpha is small. Report separate TD/retention
losses, preclip total/branch/V gradient norms and clip frequency. Target
parameters must receive no gradients. Do not tune lambda/alpha/rho after
fixtures or research outcomes. These constants are prospective choices,
not established optimal settings.

DDQN exposure proposed unchanged:50episodes/fit,2000slots,warmup128,
1872updates,uniform replay4096,batch64,Adam0.0001,betas(.9,.999),eps1e-8,
weight_decay0,target copy each200updates,9scheduled copies. Epsilon is1
before slot128,else1-.95*min(slot/2000,1),using zero-based slots. The same
first128random slots are deliberately retained to isolate this correction;
exploration resets/reward redesign would require a different amendment.
Retention changes training objective/cost, not information at deployment.

8. PROPOSED COMPARISON: TWO MECHANISMS AND REQUIRED CONTROLS
All seven learned arms use the same533->24->161architecture.
A primary: calibration (scaling+value fit) AND retention, centered DDQN.
B no-retention: same calibration, DDQN with L_TD only.
C no-calibration: raw BC weights, centered DDQN with L_TD+L_keep,T=1.
D neither: raw BC weights, centered DDQN with L_TD only (failed design).
E BC-only: independently repeat BC AND calibration, then STOP; no online
  rollout, no TD replay/optimizer. Must match A/B handoff tensor hashes.
F previous baseline: old -softplus(b+sum z) BC+DDQN, unchanged BC and TD,
  no calibration or retention; identical raw initialization/demos.
G plain small DDQN: old -softplus formulation, no BC, no demo replay,
  ordinary empty replay and same online budget; identical raw initialization.
Reference: evaluate the unchanged observed-load/radio teacher once.

A/B/C/D form a2x2comparison. Calibration bundles rescaling and V fitting:
this study cannot causally isolate those two substages. Initial algebra and
first-update diagnostics address their separate properties; a later claim
that one substage alone caused policy gains would need its own ablation.
E isolates benefit of DDQN beyond the same starting policy. F checks whether
this correction actually improves the preserved previous baseline. G reports
benefit beyond ordinary small DDQN; beating G alone is insufficient.
Do not remove E/F or select the best initialization to obtain a success.

10. COMPUTATIONAL COMPLEXITY AND FAIRNESS
Deployed architecture unchanged:16841parameters,67364FP32parameter bytes,
20inherited buffer bytes. Affine branch scale and V fit are folded into weights.
Neural forward cost for dimensions d=533,h=24,B=32,K=5:
  O(d*h + h*(B*K+1)); additive screen/decode/selected-Q O(B*K).
Dense-layer weight products per forward:533*24+24*161=16656 (bias/ReLU,
validation,screen/decoding and framework overhead additional).
Calibration FP64 feature regression costs O(N*h^2+h^3) plus feature passes;
retention adds a demonstration forward/backward per TD update. Report wall
time,optimizer steps,teacher generation,replay/storage and any export work.
No fewer-training-cost claim is assumed; it is likely more expensive to train.

Measure complete validation,screen,forward,action selection,selected Q if in
the chosen interface,and output copy in the SAME timing boundary for every
controller. Match threads/precision/backend/order/warmup and retain raw times.
Proposed128states*3rounds*22policies =8448measured calls,32warmups/path/round.
All7arms have the SAME parameter storage,so beating a same-size arm cannot
establish storage reduction. Stronger compact64/wide128 and relevant
BDQ/DQfD-style references, matched information/budget disclosures, and a final
untouched evaluation are required for a broader low-complexity contribution.
Their comparisons are DEFERRED, not presumed wins. No UAV energy/latency
budget has been verified; laptop latency is not real-UAV hardware evidence.

Dueling Network Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06581
State-value/advantage separation is established. We cannot claim its invention.

Deep Q-learning from Demonstrations (DQfD):
https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.03732
Explicitly combines TD with supervised classification of demonstrator actions.
Our continuous imitation loss overlaps that established principle. A smaller
architecture plus demos alone does not establish a new algorithmic contribution.

Policy Distillation:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.06295
Compact students extracting teacher policy are established; compression and
teacher-free deployment alone are not an unexplored contribution.

Kickstarting Deep Reinforcement Learning:
https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.03835
Teacher-guided student RL training overlaps the aim of useful initialization
and continued teacher guidance. Our fixed retention rule differs from the
abstract's adaptive teacher-use approach but novelty is not established.

Potential narrow distinction to investigate: a transparent policy-preserving
affine handoff that ties an additive critic's initial action gaps and scalar
values to recorded offloading returns, with teacher-free compact inference.
This exact composition has NOT been established as absent from prior work.
Require a full-text and UAV-IoV-specific literature comparison before claiming
novelty. The earlier2025DQfD-DECO overlap also remains unresolved; no claim
that this branch is first in UAV-IoV or that the degree requirement is met.
