DESIGN AND CLAIM DECISION AFTER THE RQ24 COST FAILURE
Date: 2026-11-19 (Asia/Shanghai)
Decision level: SAVED-EVIDENCE DESIGN ASSESSMENT ONLY

MAIN DECISION
Park RQ24-v1 and its equivalent optimized runtime as preserved unsuccessful
cost-gate candidates. Do not start their research training or automatically
try more implementation variants. Our goal remains an implemented efficient
DDQN offloading improvement, but the full low-complexity claim is unproven.

There is a credible OPERATION-COUNT argument for assessing a different,
aggregate radio-interaction surrogate. There is no measured proof that it
will meet latency, policy, calibration or novelty requirements. Recommend
only a prospective design/algebra gate for that ONE alternative. It is a
semantic redesign, not an equivalent optimization or a pass of RQ24-v1.

1. WHAT THE CURRENT EVIDENCE DOES AND DOES NOT SAY
The original completed ADP development run is audited and archived. It
beats small/wide DDQN controls on several policy comparisons, but fails
all combined predeclared scientific screens. The observed-load/radio
heuristic remains stronger. Whole-horizon calibration remains poor.

RQ24 was designed to address the diagnosed radio/deadline weakness. Its
prototype algebra/autograd/export fixtures pass; NO research policy was
trained for RQ24. Its original and optimized complete median costs fail.
Correctness is not policy success, and untrained inference is not a failed
training result. These stages must not be merged in the thesis narrative.

The optimized runtime reduced its own median by11.60-13.20%, but is still
1.72-1.80 times the unmasked wide128 median in its matched synthetic test.
Parameter reduction is supported for this architecture. Faster-than-wide
inference and equal/better policy are not supported for this candidate.

2. THE PRACTICAL LATENCY BUDGET
Same-session optimized-v1 benchmark, milliseconds:
fit | screened plain24 | unmasked wide128 | optimized RQ24
0   | .02690           | .03450           | .06100
1   | .02660           | .03530           | .06080
2   | .02700           | .03430           | .06170

Wide-minus-small margin: .00760,.00870,.00730ms (7.3-8.7 microseconds).
This difference is not an independent mechanism allowance or an exact
lower bound: networks, values and decoders differ and timings are noisy.
It is a useful warning against adding an elaborate physical optimizer.

The earlier ORIGINAL-RQ24 component profile put feature/graph work near
.031ms, network near .017ms and decoder near .011ms. These overlap and
are not exact additive shares. They were NOT a profile of optimized-v1;
do not subtract them from optimized-v1 to claim an attainable new latency.

Reaching the SAME-SESSION unmasked-wide median from optimized-v1 would
require reductions of about43.4%,41.9%,44.4% in fits0,1,2. No evidence
supports expecting that from scalar-loop cleanup alone.

3. OPTIONS CONSIDERED (NO NEW CONTROLLERS IMPLEMENTED)

A. Reduce K or remove only some radio edges.
   Removes pair coefficients/adjacency work but retains geometry, channel
   functions, unaries, network and coordinate decoding. Can miss interacting
   vehicles; small K does not bound undirected node degree. Choosing K after
   inspecting this outcome would need a new declared design. No measured
   reason to expect this alone closes the gap. Do not pursue as a rescue.

B. Unary-only physical DDQN (no assignment interaction).
   Removes graph construction and pair rate calculations, but retains much
   feature overhead. It cannot directly express the new joint interference
   cost when multiple tasks choose a UAV. Existing no-pair ablation already
   gives a way to test that loss in a future comparison. This is not the
   preferred next contribution hypothesis and not a proven fast alternative.

C. Fixed-pass aggregate radio-interaction DDQN.
   Replace per-edge exact two-signal rate increments with cached aggregates
   of cheap geometry/payload/slack features. No edge lists, donor selection,
   trigonometric path-loss model or logarithmic radio rate per pair.
   Preserve a joint assignment interaction rather than simply deleting it.
   This is the ONE proposed direction for a design/algebra gate below.

D. Distill the observed-load/radio heuristic into a small network.
   Could move heavy computation into training, but distillation/imitation
   is established and introduces teacher-label cost and attribution issues.
   It would need comparisons against plain imitation, DDQN and the teacher.
   It is not selected alongside C; do not open a second implementation track.

E. Claim parameter-efficient DDQN with an absolute latency bound instead
   of faster-than-wide inference.
   Potentially a legitimate different empirical claim IF independently
   justified and supported by policy and calibration evidence. It is a
   prospective topic/claim decision, not a relabeling of failed criteria.
   The present record does not authorize relaxing the speed requirement.

4. AGGREGATE RADIO CANDIDATE: ALGEBRA TO ASSESS, NOT A FROZEN MODEL
Let i index current vehicle tasks and d index UAV destinations. Use only
observed current positions, payload B_i, relative deadline tau_i and the
existing task/queue features. Define a simple dimensionless link proxy
  g_id = H^2 / (horizontal_distance_id^2 + H^2),
  x_id = (B_i/B_max) * (.300/tau_i) / g_id,
  y_id = g_id.
Use the existing UAV altitude H and task payload scale B_max. This g is a
geometry proxy, NOT the current simulator's channel gain; it has no LoS/
NLoS model, channel calibration or radio-rate guarantee. Valid task deadlines
are positive. Battery and movement limitations must be explicitly retained.

For the currently selected assignments define
  X_d = sum_{i:a_i=d} x_id,
  Y_d = sum_{i:a_i=d} y_id,
  Z_d = sum_{i:a_i=d} x_id*y_id,
  P_radio = sum_d lambda_d(s) * (X_d*Y_d-Z_d)/N, lambda_d>=0.
Equivalently, each same-UAV unordered pair contributes
  lambda_d*(x_id*y_jd+x_jd*y_id)/N.
There are no explicit stored pair coefficients. The value is nonnegative
algebraically; numerical cancellation needs an explicit implementation rule
and tests before this can become a specified critic. Do not silently clamp
values in a way that invalidates the insertion-marginal identity.

After removing task i, the exact insertion marginal for THIS surrogate is
  Delta_radio(i,d)=lambda_d*(x_id*Y_minus_i,d + y_id*X_minus_i,d)/N.
X and Y can be updated in constant work per move. The cost models the
qualitative effect of strong transmitters burdening weaker receivers and
gives payload/deadline-sensitive weights. It does NOT calculate actual
multiuser rates, event overlaps, queue completion or deadline feasibility.

The future brief must specify how this term combines with compute queue
costs, signed neural residuals and the scalar value transform, then use ONE
consistent scalar for decoder decisions and DDQN same-action evaluation.
Do not add a repair heuristic at inference while training a different Q.
Architecture width/output layout and scalar precision are NOT frozen here;
no claimed parameter count or tested model is assigned to this candidate.

5. WHY IT COULD COST LESS, AND WHAT IT LOSES
Radio feature preparation and exact aggregate marginals require O(U*N)
feature/storage work, with no explicit O(U*N*K) graph construction or
hundreds of rate-log evaluations. For fixed U,D, a single coordinate pass
costs O(N*D). If decreasing-cycle order is retained, include O(N log N)
sorting; do not call the whole algorithm strictly linear without handling
that cost. The small neural forward and validation remain measured costs.

This operation-count reduction is credible, but the previous ADP decoder
already missed its own strict median criterion near the wide-reference
cost. Similar aggregate bookkeeping might still be too slow. There is no
timing estimate promoted to a measured result and no promised pass.

Lost fidelity relative to RQ24-v1: reference-rate magnitude, LoS/NLoS
attenuation, nonlinear pair rate increments and deadline hinges based on
estimated upload/compute completion. No per-edge sparsification can be
blamed for omissions because all same-destination interactions are folded
into an APPROXIMATE low-rank sum instead. Distant/weak links and geometry
asymmetry may be misranked; UAV positions change during slots; compressed
queues and old pending uploads remain only partially observed. A learned
residual may fail to compensate. Lower operation count may worsen policy.

6. NOVELTY AND CALIBRATION RISKS
Pair-factorized Q functions, low-rank factors, congestion potentials and
coordinate optimization already exist (see DCG and the saved closest-method
review). This rank-two-style aggregate is not a newly invented mathematical
factorization. Radio/deadline-aware offloading also exists. No new first-
of-its-kind or publication-ready novelty claim follows from this memo.

Potential application contribution, ONLY after evidence: a compact DDQN
with a precisely specified inexpensive radio/queue assignment mechanism
that improves the measured policy/cost tradeoff against matched controls.
The mechanism-removal ablation and fixed-physics decoder must demonstrate
that learning and the proposed interaction add something. If plain small
DDQN or the analytic controller matches it, mechanism-specific novelty is
unsupported. Adapted DCG/low-rank methods need explicit overlap assessment.

The proposed aggregate does not solve the existing return-to-go calibration
or terminal-anchor gradient conflict. Retaining a horizon-scaled critic may
retain those problems. No repeated normalized-loss experiment, clipping
relaxation or bigger training budget is implied. The next brief must keep
calibration as an independent measured requirement and state this risk.

7. NEXT BOUNDED GATE AND STOP RULE
Prepare one prospective aggregate-radio DDQN brief plus an analytical
complexity/closest-work comparison. Specify every value equation, decoder
tie rule, initialization, movement behavior, numerical rule, DDQN target,
loss and mechanism ablation BEFORE implementing it. Explicitly identify
changes from ADP and RQ24; do not present it as the same frozen experiment.

Only after that brief: a small synthetic algebra and complete matched-cost
gate can decide whether research preparation is worthwhile. Require direct
pair-sum/aggregate equality, cached-marginal/direct-delta equality, surrogate
nonincrease, legal actions, edge-case arithmetic, gradients and same-backend
references. Preserve all measurements. Failure means stop and review, not
automatic K/width/loss tuning until something passes.

No new development task/model seeds or research budget are assigned here.
If synthetic cost passes, fresh-seed protocol preparation, runner/auditor
fixtures and a separate explicit research launch decision remain necessary.
Final reservations stay untouched. Do not revise the Word thesis to claim
success. Security/trust deferred; cloud/charging excluded.

8. WHAT WE CAN SAY TODAY
We have an implemented offloading research pipeline, audited comparative
evidence, a diagnosed policy bottleneck and verified parameter reductions.
We do not yet have a controller satisfying the combined speed, policy,
value-stability and mechanism criteria. This is a hard design tradeoff,
not a laptop limitation or a lack of implementation. This memo recommends
one bounded hypothesis assessment without promising that it will succeed.
