ONE PROPOSED CORRECTION: FINITE-HORIZON TERMINAL ANCHORING
Retain the Version3 priced architecture and its action decoder. Let
C_theta(s,a) be the existing nonnegative additive placement, movement,
bias and workload-price cost. Let H=40, and t be the discrete slot index
recovered from the existing normalized time input. Define h(s)=H-t for
nonterminal states, and h(s)=0 at terminal states.

Candidate critic:
Q_theta(s,a) = -h(s) * C_theta(s,a).
For plain24/compact controls, use h(s)*Q_base(s,a) with their original
nonpositive output definitions. No extra model parameters are introduced.
At a fixed state h>0, scaling does not change the greedy action of the same
weights. It changes training gradients and learned weights. At h=0 the
value is exactly zero; do not call the decoder on terminal states.
This enforces horizon scaling and terminal boundary zero, not a finite lower
bound or correct last-slot calibration. Learned C can still become too large.
No mathematical bounded-value or optimality claim is made.

Keep DDQN selection/evaluation:
a_star = argmax_a Q_online(s_next,a)
y = r + (1-done)*gamma*Q_target(s_next,a_star), gamma=1.
Done handling must skip next-state decoding exactly as in the tested runner.
Uniform-replay DDQN loss remains mean Huber(Q(s,a)-y), beta=1.

Add one separately normalized terminal anchor to the objective:
L = L_uniform_DDQN + 1.0 * L_terminal,
where L_terminal is the mean Huber(Q(s_T,a_T)-r_T) over a separate sample
of32 terminal transitions, sampled uniformly with replacement from stored
terminal transitions. If none are available, record an empty-anchor event
and use zero anchor loss; never fabricate terminal data. Do not add optimizer
steps: combine both terms before the existing single backward/update.
Terminal transitions are already in replay; keep a parallel index list and
verify eviction handling. No additional environment transitions are generated.
The coefficient1.0 and batch32 are prospective fixed choices for engineering
review, not tuned against subsequent outcomes. This changes the training
objective explicitly and must not be described as unchanged uniform DDQN.

Decoder and complexity:
At h>0 the original exact separable argmin remains valid; use original stable
tie behavior. Complexity remains neural forward O(d*w+w*k) plus O(N*K)
placement argmins and two motion argmins, with N=30,K=5,w=24,d=533.
Horizon scaling adds constant work. Terminal sampling/loss adds training work
and replay bookkeeping. Measure total training time and replay storage too;
do not describe it as computationally free. Inference/storage must be measured
on the complete frozen deployment path after retraining.

Horizon conditioning, terminal replay and DDQN are established ideas. This
brief proposes an experimentally testable correction to the current controller;
it does not establish literature novelty. Any eventual contribution remains
the measured efficient offloading controller and its justified pricing
mechanism, contingent on successful comparisons and a literature check.
An implementation plus ablation evidence is necessary, but does not alone
guarantee thesis sufficiency or supervisor acceptance.
