ADP-DDQN: PROSPECTIVE LITERATURE POSITION AND CLAIM LIMITS
Date: 2026-11-15

This stage re-inspected saved full-text evidence and the prior closest-work
matrix. No new live retrieval or exhaustive novelty search was performed.
Publication/index currency and access gaps therefore remain. A comparison
specification can be frozen while novelty clearance remains explicitly open;
it cannot be treated as thesis novelty confirmation or research launch release.

Known overlap (saved full texts):
1. Action Branching Architectures for Deep Reinforcement Learning,
   arXiv:1711.08946: shared branches, DDQN/BDQ and linear action-output growth
   already exist. Keep adapted BDQ and same-width plain-DDQN references.
2. Sustainable Task Offloading in Secure UAV-assisted Smart Farm Networks:
   A Multi-Agent DRL with Action Mask Approach, arXiv:2407.19657: DDQN with
   action masking already exists in UAV offloading. Generic DDQN+mask is not
   the proposed new contribution. That work's security does not validate ours.
3. Vehicular offloading, arXiv:2404.07215: saved text explicitly describes
   simultaneous terminal choices causing server/channel congestion and DDQN
   request scheduling. Queue/congestion awareness alone is not distinct.
4. Hierarchical UAV/vehicular edge offloading, arXiv:2507.05722: resource-aware
   node scoring/scheduling with learned control exists. A learned network plus
   deterministic allocator is not new as a generic combination.
5. FlexEdge, arXiv:2305.01536, and saved local TMC 2024 full text
   DOI10.1109/TMC.2024.3350078 discuss joint offloading/trajectory and complexity.
   Measuring model size or runtime is necessary evidence, not novelty itself.

Candidate-specific hypothesis: jointly learn a compact nonpositive coupled
action-value potential whose exact insertion marginal depends on ACTUAL
current assignment workload, with a fixed single coordinate sweep used for
approximate online DDQN action selection and same-action target evaluation.
The (1-eta) sum(w_i^2) self-term preserves the old single-task marginal while
eta weights assigned cross terms. See frozen design equations. This algebra
and coordinate descent draw on established congestion/potential methods.
Do not claim to invent quadratic congestion costs, marginal pricing, coordinate
descent, value anchoring or approximate greedy selection in RL.

Testable differences, not novelty clearance:
- A versus coupled_no_sweep isolates the sweep within the coupled critic.
- A versus separable64 changes coupled objective and selection; not sweep alone.
- A versus fixed_price_plain_sweep tests learned price/mixture heads against
  fixed beta1/eta.2 with the same coupling and sweep.
- The observed-state radio/load heuristic tests a no-learning, physically
  informed alternative under explicit approximation/movement limitations.
- Same-width plain24, compact64, wide128 and BDQ prevent gains being credited
  solely to network size or a weak reference.
Policy/calibration results cannot retrospectively redefine which distinction
was primary. A positive computational screen alone does not answer these tests.

Outstanding before novelty can be asserted: obtain and compare full methods
for exact-assignment congestion potentials, learned cost-to-go/marginal resource
allocation and fixed-pass approximate DDQN decoders; search beyond the existing
domain set. Q-TOP DOI10.1109/TVT.2026.3721145 and deadline-aware dependent-task
offloading DOI10.1109/TVT.2026.3676992 still have metadata/abstract evidence only
in the saved matrix. Their absent full texts do not prove an absent mechanism.
No claim of first-of-its-kind, no publication-grade novelty clearance, and no
cross-paper numerical performance comparison is made here.
