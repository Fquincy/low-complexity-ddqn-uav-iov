PROFILE AND IMPLEMENTATION
The saved original-runtime profile covers 128 calls after 32 warmups, on the
saved engineering coupled_sweep_0 checkpoint. It is diagnostic profiling, not
a replacement acceptance timing. Instrumented cumulative time: heads 0.067 s,
potential 0.028 s, allowed 0.027 s, remaining 0.020 s (nested inside heads),
native decode 0.003 s, overall 0.157 s. Do not add nested cumulative times or
interpret these instrumented durations as production latency. Preparation,
validation and repeated value evaluation dominate this observed profile;
the coordinate sweep is not the principal measured overhead in that trace.

assignment_price_fast_runtime.py creates an immutable inference snapshot:
- Script/freeze the same PyTorch neural/head operations, retaining the FP32
  transforms and FP64 potential/decoder inputs. No neural architecture change.
- Compile finite-state/time-grid validation and the local-feasibility mask,
  retaining outward rounding and adjacent-FP32 intervals.
- Reuse the existing native decoder and its full coupled cost instead of
  constructing another one-hot tensor potential for selected actions.
- Retain independent motion, stable ordering, old-action exact ties, masks,
  finite-horizon factor and coupled/no-sweep/separable/fixed-price variants.
Training definitions, DDQN targets and objectives in the original prototype
are unchanged. Training must rebuild the snapshot after weight updates;
this runtime is not a live view of mutable optimizer weights.

CORRECTNESS
Four test groups passed:
1. All four prototype arms, both saved engineering initializations and all
   128 shared states: 1,024 model/state checks. All seven prepared heads
   match exactly; actions match exactly; selected values agree at the existing
   atol 1e-6 / rtol 1e-5. Original model weights remain unchanged.
2. 500 randomized/adjoining-boundary mask and remaining-horizon checks.
3. Rejection of nonfinite, off-grid/terminal, invalid task and queue states,
   wrong dtype/shape, plus noncontiguous input equivalence.
4. Equal-head ties, returned-array ownership and snapshot independence from
   subsequent training-model mutations.
Native decode is the original tested function, preserving its earlier one-ULP,
coordinate-oracle and counterexample tests. This does not prove equivalence
on all possible numerical inputs or robustness after research training.

LIMITS AND NEXT TECHNICAL STEP
Zero simulator steps, optimizer updates or research training in this stage.
No return/deadline evidence; final evaluation seeds and Word thesis untouched.
The libraries emit TorchScript deprecation warnings; the tested environment
and saved graph artifacts are retained. A future runtime migration requires
new equivalence evidence. Model tensor payload does not include shared library
code or process peak memory; first-action records share import/compilation
caches and are not fresh-process cold start. Actual peak memory is unmeasured.

Keep the controller equations, masks, precision and thresholds fixed. The next
bounded step is diagnostic profiling of the optimized route, then deciding
whether a fully fused deployment route can remove remaining tensor/framework
dispatch at unchanged action/value semantics. It has not yet been profiled,
so the current evidence does not identify its remaining dominant operation.
If proceeding, declare a new version and benchmark before measuring; retain
both failed gates. Do not launch a simulator fixture or research experiment
while this cost gate fails. This route does not solve the separate outstanding
novelty, policy-quality or value-calibration questions.
