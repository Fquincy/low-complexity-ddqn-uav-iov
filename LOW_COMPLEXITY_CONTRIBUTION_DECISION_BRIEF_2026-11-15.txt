2. THE INTENDED CONTRIBUTION
Working contribution statement, conditional on future evidence:
  A compact DDQN offloading controller combining a decomposed joint-action
  critic, a workload-dependent marginal-cost term and conservative local
  feasibility screening, with measured reductions in online parameter storage
  and complete action-selection cost while retaining competitive return and
  deadline success against equally informed compact, wide and heuristic controls.

This is an implemented controller-improvement claim within the approved
offloading problem. Simulation supplies the evaluation evidence. It is not
merely a proposal to create an experiment, and it is not yet a proven claim.
Use "compact" as a factual architecture description and "measured efficiency"
with named comparators. Decide whether "lightweight" or "low complexity" is
the stronger defensible thesis wording only after the specified evidence.
Neither label automatically follows from a small parameter count.

No claim here that DDQN, branching, action masks, terminal anchoring or loss
normalization is new. The candidate novelty is the specific controller
mechanism/implementation for this offloading problem and a demonstrated useful
performance-cost tradeoff beyond simpler alternatives. That distinction needs
closest-paper comparison and mechanism evidence; integration alone is not
automatically sufficient novelty. Thesis sufficiency is not guaranteed.

3. WHAT EXISTS AS AN IMPLEMENTATION
N=30 vehicles, five destination choices, two UAV motion branches with five
choices, 533 observed features, hidden width24 and a40-slot finite horizon.
For the original screened price controller, write its deployed critic as
  Q_theta(s,a) = -h(s)[ b_theta(s) + sum_i g_theta,i,a_i(s)
                                      + sum_j v_theta,j,a_j(s) ],
  h(s)=40-round(40*s[-1]).
g contains the implemented nonnegative task cost and workload-price marginal
term; b and v use the existing nonnegative parameterization. Screening only
excludes a local choice certified infeasible using outward observation bounds
on task cycles/deadline and local CPU service time. All remote/motion choices
remain available. Remote deadlines are not guaranteed. The screen does not
guarantee zero local misses near uncertainty boundaries or under queueing.

The decoder independently minimizes the stored allowed branch-cost tables.
It is exact for this DECOMPOSED learned critic, not the true coupled scheduling
problem. Actual offloading actions interact through shared radio/CPU queues.
This structural approximation is a possible limitation, not a solved optimality
claim. Workload prices use observations; private task completion labels and
future workloads are never policy inputs.

Original DDQN update remains:
  a_next=argmax_allowed Q_online(s_next,a),
  y=r+Q_target(s_next,a_next) if nonterminal, otherwise y=r (gamma1),
  L=mean Huber_1(Q_online(s,a)-stopgrad(y))
        +1.0*mean_terminal Huber_1(Q_online(s,a)-r).
Uniform batch128, terminal batch32 and the existing optimizer schedule remain
as documented in their frozen protocols. This brief changes no formula.

For fixed width and destination count, the branch decoder scales linearly
with vehicle count; the network input/output sizes also grow with vehicle
count. The present timing evidence is atN=30. It does not demonstrate empirical
scalability across system sizes or superior asymptotic complexity to branching
or additive controls, which also avoid exponential joint-action enumeration.

4. EVIDENCE LEDGER: SUPPORT AND LIMITS
A. IMPLEMENTED EFFICIENCY: supported under the tested configuration.
Primary online parameters:17,066;68,264 FP32 bytes plus20 buffer bytes.
Original compact64:194,944 parameter bytes; wide128:422,016 bytes.
The primary has64.98% fewer parameter bytes than compact64 and83.82% fewer
than wide128. Report full action selection, buffers, deployment files, startup
and training cost separately. CPU timings are host/backend/input-specific;
do not present them as UAV hardware, total device memory or energy savings.

B. SCREENING EFFECT: positive audited DEVELOPMENT evidence.
The earlier deadline-screen primary improved five-fit mean return by11.297719
and deadline success by24.055208 percentage points over its unmasked counterpart.
Every paired initialization improved both. In the later horizon-loss study,
the original-loss screened control again beat its unmasked price counterpart
in both metrics in all five fits. This later assessment is exploratory control
evidence, not promotion to primary or independent untouched final confirmation.

C. PRACTICAL POLICY QUALITY: promising, not complete proof.
On the later fresh study's consumed development set, the original-loss screened
price control achieved deadline success96.1337%,95.8247%,96.4392%,99.6441%,97.6250%
and episode returns -15.2959,-15.5172,-15.8633,-13.6312,-14.1842.
It exceeded original compact64, both wide128 variants, ordinary plain24,
adapted BDQ and both strong heuristics in both metrics in every fit in that
study. These are observed sampled results, not deployment reliability bounds.
The earlier run had weaker fits; do not silently discard the earlier variation.

D. DISTINCT PRICE/ARCHITECTURE ADVANTAGE: unresolved.
In the later study the original-price controller lost both metrics to original
no-price in fits0,1,2. It also missed the same policy margin versus screened,
anchored plain24 in fit0. Its large mean advantage over no-price is strongly
affected by no-price fit4's poor result. Means alone cannot establish consistent
price benefit. Plain24 also has fewer parameters. A more complicated price
term needs positive evidence or a defensible reason to retain it.

E. GLOBAL VALUE CONSISTENCY: unresolved.
All five original-price fits in the later study failed the normalized-RMSE
limit.25; values were1.1299,.4016,1.1094,.2559,1.4157. Absolute biases were
8.7879,1.9807,9.2103,.9511,10.2988 versus limit2. Original policy performance
does not erase these failures. The horizon-normalized primary also failed.
Return prediction calibration and useful action ranking are different properties;
a policy-efficiency claim does not logically require exact optimal-Q calibration.
However the existing protocols explicitly required these limits and remain failed.
Do not reinterpret their failure as a pass or remove limits retrospectively.

F. NORMALIZATION CORRECTION: rejected under its tested setup.
Normalized minus original: primary -7.753247 return/-13.984028 deadline points;
no-price -300.846349/-31.931944; plain -460.738173/-42.233333.
Both metrics worsened for every architecture pair and initialization. Reduced
clipping did not establish learning success. Price primary misses concentrated
at overloaded RSUs; no-price/plain had heavy late/unfinished UAV uploads.
Saved associations do not prove a single causal optimizer explanation.

G. NOVELTY AND FINAL GENERALIZATION: open.
The available literature note checked four established component papers using
limited abstracts/metadata. It explicitly is not a full-paper UAV-IoV novelty
review. Final model/task reservations remain unconsumed and protected.

5. ALTERNATIVES AND RECOMMENDATION
Route A - recommended: retain the original-loss compact screened design as
incumbent, then resolve its value interpretation and mechanism distinction.
This preserves useful implementation work and investigates the strongest
current policy candidate without pretending the failed primary succeeded.
Risk: price may not justify its added complexity, and calibration/robustness
may still prevent the intended claim. Evaluate against those risks directly.

Route B - conditional: simplify or make ONE evidence-driven controller change.
This becomes appropriate only if the next diagnosis identifies a concrete
mechanism worth testing, or finds price redundant. A simpler successful compact
controller could satisfy measured efficiency, but its novel improvement over
ordinary small DDQN must still be established. No architecture is chosen here.

Route C - retain modeling/negative experimental findings as supporting material.
These results remain useful but are not being substituted for the requested
implemented-controller contribution. They document why simpler interventions
failed and prevent unsupported thesis statements.
