LOW COMPLEXITY EMC-Q MECHANICS AND COST REVIEW
Date: November 4, 2026
Status: implemented; mechanics and local cost measurement complete.

DECISION
EMC-Q satisfies the checked mechanics and exact restricted-argmax contract.
It does NOT establish lower inference latency than either additive baseline.
Do not start a long learning pilot or call this a proven low-complexity method.
The next decision is whether to pursue a narrower parameter-storage/quality
tradeoff, or optimize the exact decoder before any learning smoke. Quality
has not been tested; no reward, simulator equation or Word thesis was changed.

IMPLEMENTED
New emc_q.py:533->64->64 encoder;477 output values;20 learned movement
embedding scalars. The model represents movement-conditioned destination
interactions with rank2. Conditional on each of25 movement pairs, destination
terms are separable. Decoder maximizes those terms and picks the best pair.
One physical action vector is returned; no beam or proposal head is needed.
The decoder is exact for this restricted Q representation, not optimal control
for the physical simulator. It does not capture all destination-destination
interactions or resolve hidden-queue observation aliasing.

MECHANICS
Six tests passed:60 small random batched cases matched exhaustive action
enumeration; selected-action value equals decoded maximum; batch/single
parity; deterministic ties and interaction ablation; legal action layout; all
output blocks/embeddings and encoder receive gradients; exactly one encoder
call in greedy selection; detached DDQN targets with frozen target weights;
absorbing successor NaN sentinels skipped and no successor gradients.
No optimizer step occurred. Backward-only tests checked the loss graph.

COMPLETE-ACTION COST
Untrained models, CPU float32, one intra-op and inter-op thread.128 common
recorded observations; three rounds with randomized block order;32 warmups
then128 timed calls per path per round.384 calls/path,1536 total raw rows.
Timing includes state conversion, complete action selection and native action
dictionary decoding. Environment stepping, file IO and assertions excluded.
Weights remained unchanged and every measured action matched its untimed
reference. This is a local engineering microbenchmark, not deployment proof.

EMC median / additive128 median: 3.09x.
EMC median / additive64 median: 3.25x.
Online parameter bytes reduction vs additive128: 34.26%.
Online parameter bytes increase vs additive64: 42.32%.
Median latency reduction vs historical SPI: 93.71%.

The compact additive ablation is both smaller and faster. EMC may still earn
its additional cost through higher policy quality, but that is untested.
Do not attribute width reduction to the interaction mechanism. Historical SPI
is included solely as a cost reference; its flawed learning smoke is not a
quality baseline. Only same-run ratios are relevant here; older absolute
timings were taken under different runtime conditions and are not speedup
evidence across dates. CPU scheduling/power/thermal conditions were not
controlled. Peak memory, inference energy and training cost were not measured.
The replay buffer has not been implemented for EMC; no replay-memory claim.

NEXT GATE
First freeze the intended claim: smaller online parameter storage than the
128x128 reference while preserving predeclared physical return/deadline
quality, with the measured latency tradeoff disclosed; or lower latency, which
the current implementation does not achieve. Include additive64 as a required
ablation. Set quality margins and cost limits before training; no favorable
post-hoc tradeoff. If pursuing latency, profile/optimize the exact tensor
decoder under these unchanged mechanics tests before a learning experiment.
Any later smoke must use disjoint episode blocks, identical physical budgets,
independent action/replay RNGs, detached targets, final episode metrics, frozen
source snapshots and fresh greedy evaluation. A mechanics pass is not a
quality pass. No learning smoke or full pilot launched by this step.
