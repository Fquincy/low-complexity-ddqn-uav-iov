EMC-Q PAIRED RUNNER AND DISJOINT SMOKE REVIEW
Date: November 4, 2026
Scope: integration test and fixed-budget three-arm smoke only.

DECISION
The paired smoke and subsequent saved-artifact audit passed. This clears the
runner integration gate only. It does not clear the separate development-run
readiness gate and does not establish a learning, quality, low-complexity,
novelty, or deployment claim. No 72,000-slot development run was launched.

IMPLEMENTATION AND CHECKS
The separate runner is run_emc_tradeoff.py. It compares frozen additive128,
additive64, and original exact-decoder EMC-Q arms using the unchanged Chapter 3
timeline-v2 simulator. The training code uses one joint scalar Huber loss,
Double DQN online selection/target evaluation, terminal-successor skipping,
detached targets, frozen target parameters, and one update after each record
from the inclusive warmup threshold. Exploration and replay random streams are
separate, and exploration vectors are paired across arms.

Six runner tests passed. They exercised synthetic updates, target gradient and
terminal handling, paired exploration, replay wraparound, a paired physical
episode, evaluation invariance, and checkpoint round-trip. The 219 updates in
the synthetic counter test and its 120 simulator evaluation steps are test
fixtures. They are not the smoke's training totals.

The actual smoke used the frozen disjoint seed 2399 and the three specified
arms. Each arm trained for two 40-slot episodes, with warmup8, batch4,
capacity64, target copy interval8, and 73 optimizer updates/9 target copies.
Totals were 240 physical training slots and 219 updates. It also completed
18 scheduled validation episodes, 2 fixed-probe episodes, 6 final-model fresh
episodes, and 6 heuristic fresh episodes, each40 slots. That is 1,280
evaluation slots, in addition to the training slots. Timing used 5x128
measured selections per arm plus warmup; it did not step the simulator.

The runner wrote all episode trajectories/task records, physical replay
transitions, optimizer rows, and final model/target/optimizer/RNG/replay
checkpoints. It froze and hashed all final model checkpoints before fresh-gate
evaluation. Paired predecision task fingerprints, exploration histories, and
learner-state digests around scheduled validation, fresh evaluation, timing,
and fixed probes were checked.

An independent read-only artifact audit then checked 219 optimizer rows,
reconstructed replay contents and both RNG streams from the saved evidence,
verified all checkpoint/source/spec hashes, reconciled all 32 saved episodes'
task/reward/energy accounting, checked shared workloads, recomputed timing
quantiles and parameter/replay/optimizer storage totals, and matched 18
pre/post evaluation-state digests. The auditor launched zero new simulator
steps and zero optimizer updates. The refreshed audit is at
validation/emc_tradeoff_smoke_2026-11-04_03/saved_artifact_audit.json.

LIMITS OF SMOKE OUTCOMES
The two fresh seeds per model are plumbing checks only. For context, EMC-Q's
two fresh episode returns were -565.07 and -592.27, with deadline fractions
0.3750 and 0.3867. Additive128 returned -353.63 and -377.06, with fractions
0.5033 and 0.4867. These short runs are not evidence for ranking trained
policies: each model had only 80 training slots and 73 updates, and only one
initialization. Do not use these results to select, retune, or relabel an arm.

This smoke measured online parameter storage at 422,016 bytes for additive128,
194,944 for additive64, and 277,444 for EMC-Q. Those architecture sizes were
already known and do not demonstrate policy quality. Smoke timing medians were
0.0890ms, 0.0843ms, and 0.2994ms respectively; p95 values were 0.1543ms,
0.1163ms, and 0.6382ms. These small-run measurements are diagnostic and do not
replace the pre-run timing profile or establish a device-independent runtime.

DEVELOPMENT-GATE STATUS
The separate development reporting path is implemented and reviewed in
LOW_COMPLEXITY_EMC_DEVELOPMENT_RUNNER_REVIEW_2026-11-04.txt. Its tests verify

architecture, budgets, or thresholds using smoke outcomes.
