1. Deep Coordination Graphs, arXiv1910.00091v4
Retrieved full16-page PDF; methods on PDF pages3-4 and Algorithms1-3 in
appendix inspected. Rendered method page4 checked against extracted text.
Eq3-4: max-sum messages repeated for a fixed iteration count; graph cycles
do not imply a convergence guarantee. Eq5 low-rank learned pair payoffs;
Eq6 averages individual utilities and symmetric pair payoffs. Shared RNN
histories and pair networks; Q-learning with double selection/evaluation.
Rq24 difference: physical sparse same-UAV pair costs with a learned common
scale and signed unary residuals; one coordinate sweep with cached exact
surrogate deltas. No learned general action-by-action payoff matrix or RNN.
LIMIT: factorized Q, shared parameters, sparse interaction representations
and approximate greedy decoding are established. Our restricted physical
form is not automatically a new coordination-graph method.

4. Joint Task Offloading and Resource Optimization in NOMA-based Vehicular
Edge Computing: A Game-Theoretic DRL Approach, arXiv2209.12749v2
Retrieved full45-page PDF; system/method passages reviewed. PDF page13
