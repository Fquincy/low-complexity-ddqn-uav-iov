LOW COMPLEXITY BEAM-COVERAGE REVIEW
Date: 2026-11-01
Status: completed; full pilot remains blocked.

The saved SPI smoke checkpoints were evaluated on128 common observations with
three bounded decoding references: top2/beam8 (current), top3/beam32 and
top5/beam128. No optimizer updates or simulator steps were performed.

Replicate | top2=top3 action | top2=top5 action | top3=top5 action
0 | 9.38% | 0.00% | 3.91%
1 | 17.97% | 0.00% | 1.56%

The current top2/beam8 action never matched the wider top5/beam128 action in
either saved replicate. The top3/beam32 action also rarely matched top5/beam128.
This shows that the bounded decoder is highly sensitive to candidate-set width
for these learned smoke checkpoints. It does not prove that the wider action is
physically better, because no full simulator counterfactual was run here; it
does prove that beam8 is not a stable approximation to the model's own wider
joint-scoring search.

Candidate counts were exactly8,32 and128 per observation. The current trained
smoke model's joint-score differences between widths were small, but small
score differences can change the selected joint vector. The previous smoke
candidate count average below8 was caused by duplicate beam vectors, not by a
validated coverage guarantee.

DECISION
Do not launch a full SPI training pilot with top2/beam8. The low-complexity
claim is blocked by an unresolved decoding approximation. Before another
learning budget, change the architecture or define a candidate-set objective
that is explicit and measurable. Options are: increase beam width and accept
higher cost; design a single-pass scorer whose action set is directly evaluated
without beam search; or predeclare a bounded candidate generator and validate
its physical coverage against fresh simulator branches.

The current SPI prototype remains promising only relative to the sequential
path. It is still much slower than additive DDQN, and the paired smoke was
mixed across its two replicates. No low-complexity or policy-quality claim is
established.
