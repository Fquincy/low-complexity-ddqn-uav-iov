ASSIGNMENT-DEPENDENT PRICE PROTOTYPE: ENGINEERING REVIEW
Date: 2026-11-15

DECISION
Correctness checks PASS; complete-action median latency gate FAILS in both
engineering initializations. Hold simulator integration and research training.
The next bounded step is profiling this implementation and proposing an
equivalent inference route. This result does not establish a low-complexity
contribution or policy improvement, and does not establish that the underlying
assignment-dependent mechanism cannot work.

IMPLEMENTED
assignment_price_prototype.py implements the frozen assignment-dependent
potential, differentiable replay-action evaluation and one-pass coordinate
decoder. Initialization uses the prior expected-other price table; the sweep
uses actual assigned workload, descending observed-cycle order and retained
actions on exact ties. The chosen-action value includes the full coupled cost.
Four arms: coupled_sweep, coupled_no_sweep, separable64 and
fixed_price_plain_sweep. Network/head transforms use FP32; scalar potential
and decoder use FP64. DDQN selects online actions and evaluates them with
the target model, bypasses terminal decoding and retains original-unit Huber
loss plus terminal anchor weight one. There is no research training launcher.

The sweep is bounded approximate maximization. Neither global optimality nor
a physical deadline guarantee follows. The saved suboptimal counterexample
remains part of the engineering evidence. Standard congestion potentials and
coordinate descent are established methods; novelty remains unconfirmed.

CORRECTNESS EVIDENCE
Six tests passed, zero failures/errors, including:
- Parameter/buffer counts, masks, nonpositive values, chosen-action value
  consistency and inference weight invariance.
- Independent pairwise potential calculation and double-precision gradient
  checks, including price and mixture transforms.
- Independent full-cost coordinate oracle on 24 small assignment fixtures,
  exhaustive objective bounds, candidate counts and nonincrease; a case
  where coordinate descent is suboptimal is explicitly retained.
- Exact ties, one-ULP distinctions, adjacent-FP32 local boundary and invalid
  state/dtype handling.
- DDQN online/target handling, terminal bypass, anchor formulas and four toy
  optimizer updates with finite gradients and no target gradients.
- Eager/native/runtime action and value agreement for four arms on 128
  engineering observations, at declared value tolerances.
These tests do not cover every possible numerical input or establish training
stability. The independent oracle supplies evidence beyond comparing two
routes sharing the same decoder algorithm.

In each fit the primary passes the limit of half compact64 parameter bytes
and p95 <= 1 ms, but fails median <= each wide reference. It takes roughly
eight times the wide-reference median. A small network alone does not imply
fast complete action selection. The separable64 route also takes 0.631 and
0.66685 ms; the saved timings alone cannot identify the dominant overhead.
No component profile has established its cause. Parameter storage excludes
20 buffer bytes, FP64 heads, masks, work arrays and framework temporaries.
Theoretical workspace O(ND+N+D), plus sorting workspace; actual process peak
memory is unmeasured. First-action records share a process/import caches and
are not fresh-process deployment startup measurements.

SCOPE AND NEXT STEP
This gate used zero new simulator steps, four synthetic optimizer steps in
tests, and no research training. Cost checkpoints were untrained objects
separate from those toy updates. No return/deadline evidence was generated.
Preserve this failed cost result and the existing scientific experiments.
Keep the current mathematics, masks, tie rules and precision fixed while
profiling the runtime. Any optimization needs action/value equivalence and
a separate prospective benchmark with preserved old timings; no relaxation
of the failed thresholds. Advance to simulator fixtures only after review
of adequate cost evidence. Final evaluation seeds and the Word thesis remain
unchanged. Security/trust, cloud and charging remain outside this branch.
