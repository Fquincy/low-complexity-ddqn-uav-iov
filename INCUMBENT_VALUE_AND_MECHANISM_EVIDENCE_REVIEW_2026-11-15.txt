1. READ-ONLY CHECKPOINT CHECK
Arms: original-loss screened_price24, screened_no_price24 and
screened_plain24_anchored. All five fits,48 consumed development episodes per
fit:15 fixed models,720 episodes,28,800 states. No new task seeds or rollout.
Every input trace/array hash was checked against the saved run manifest;
checkpoints matched the final freeze. Frozen batch-one FP32 online/target
predictions reproduced saved values at unchanged atol1e-6/rtol1e-5. Executed
greedy actions and following-state actions matched exactly. Weights unchanged.
Terminal next-state decoding was bypassed; y=r at terminal transitions.
No simulator steps, optimizer updates, retiming or final-seed use occurred.

For each executed action at saved state s_t:
  e_DDQN = Q_online(s_t,a_t)-r_t-Q_target(s_next,argmax Q_online(s_next,.))
  e_self = Q_online(s_t,a_t)-r_t-Q_online(s_next,argmax Q_online(s_next,.))
Both next-state terms are zero at termination. These are fixed-checkpoint
diagnostics, not training-log TD residuals computed with evolving weights.
The full saved trajectory telescoping identity was verified:
  Q_online(s_t,a_t)-G_t = sum_(k=t..39) e_self,k.
Thus small systematic errors across several steps can yield substantial
return bias. This algebra locates the discrepancy, not its optimizer cause.
Training summaries and frozen-evaluation summaries describe different states,
actions and weights; do not compare them as identical residual distributions.

Self residual and frozen-target residual differ. This shows target-network

target lag caused the training outcome. Changing target updates is not
authorized or justified as an automatic fix by these observations.

4. WHAT THE EVIDENCE DOES NOT RESOLVE
One realized rollout is not a conditional expected optimal-Q label. The state
is a compressed observation of a queueing system; hidden histories can affect
outcomes. Target and online critics are not ground truth. These limitations
do not excuse documented sign/terminal/integrity failures or erase the old
global consistency criteria. No post-hoc offset calibration, scaling or
checkpoint selection was performed. A constant bias correction is not justified:
errors vary with horizon, fit and observations, and useful action ranking
cannot be inferred from scalar error alone.

6. GO / CHANGE / STOP DECISION
STOP the unsuccessful horizon-normalized-loss candidate under its tested setup.
CONTINUE the contribution-one goal and retain original-loss FS-DDQN as incumbent.
CHANGE the mechanism brief before another research run. Do not relabel old
failed experiments, promote a control to confirmed primary, or lower thresholds.

Recommended next bounded design question:
  Can explicitly allocation-dependent workload pricing coordinate simultaneous
  destination choices better than the current separable expected-load table,
  while retaining a measured, bounded decoding cost?

The current price table uses expected other assignments rather than the actual
joint assignment under construction. Once tables are computed, independent
branch minimization is exact only for that separable learned objective. Real
queue costs depend on simultaneous assignments. A decoder that explicitly
tracks candidate assigned load is a concrete difference from the incumbent
and plain branchwise DDQN, but NOT established as novel or beneficial here.
The observed RSU concentration motivates testing the limitation; it does not
prove missing coupling caused it. Do not claim an allocation-dependent decoder
would fix calibration or deadlines. Generic load-aware greedy allocation is
also established and must be treated as a strong baseline, not hidden novelty.

The next brief should define ONE mechanism: the exact workload potential,
observed-state inputs, action objective, deterministic allocation/order rule,
termination and worst-case work bound, DDQN behavior/next-action consistency,
and what is exact versus approximate. Keep original rewards, observation,
original-unit loss and architecture fixed initially to isolate the decoder.
No exact joint-optimality or remote deadline guarantee may be invented.
If the mechanism can be explained entirely by a simple load-aware heuristic,
or the required search is too expensive, simplify/stop that candidate before
training. Require same-information heuristic and plain-small-DDQN+same-decoder
controls, plus a mechanism-removed incumbent, before attributing benefit to
learned price architecture. Verify behavior first on isolated engineering data.
