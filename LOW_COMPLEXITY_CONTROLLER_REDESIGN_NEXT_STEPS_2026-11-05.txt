THE SINGLE CURRENT DESIGN
Use VERSION 2 of the design brief and comparison protocol.
The candidate shares a small vehicle encoder and placement head, models a
learned server-load cost and uses two parallel load-price passes followed by
four individually scored assignment repairs. The DDQN-style update uses an
online action selector and separate target evaluation with an approximate
joint decoder. The value estimate is a sum of nonnegative costs with a minus
sign; its calibration will be tested on the model's own trajectories.

The analytical parameter estimate is 4,052, compared with 48,736 for the plain
compact reference. This is not a measured memory/runtime result. Its neural
MAC count is slightly higher than that compact reference, and coordination
adds work. It must pass a complete-action runtime gate before research training.

Version 1 is kept as a superseded draft. A synthetic symmetry example exposed
price-pass oscillation; version 2 repairs that example. Forty random algebra
fixtures and 800 marginal-cost identities were checked. This is not a neural
implementation test, a full runner smoke, or evidence of improved policy quality.
