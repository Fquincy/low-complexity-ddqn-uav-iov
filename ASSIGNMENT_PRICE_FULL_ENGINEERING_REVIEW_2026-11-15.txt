Date: 2026-11-15

DECISION
Expanded heuristic, all-original-control CPU deployment and complete small
fifteen-controller fixture PASS their tests and saved-artifact audit. No
research experiment is launched. This confirms a larger engineering path,
not policy superiority, novelty, scientific acceptance or full launch readiness.
The next required gate is binding and exercising the full five-initialization
research orchestration/release against the frozen prospective specification.
The current executable remains fixture-only even with correct confirmation.

IMPLEMENTATION
assignment_price_load_radio_heuristic.py implements the frozen observed-load/
radio surrogate: observed task bits/cycles/deadlines, CPU workloads, serial
radio backlog, observed positions, public channel/CPU constants, current-
assignment UAV interference, fixed descending-cycle initialization and one
coordinate sweep. It obeys the local screen, excludes empty-battery UAVs for
new assignments, always allows RSUs and returns stationary UAV movement.
No fitting, future arrivals, exact simulator task information or env object
enters its interface. Queue/radio estimates are approximate; stationary
movement and surrogate wait/energy accounting remain declared limitations.
It is not an exact deadline/return oracle or an optimal/lightweight scheduler.

assignment_price_all_reference_deployment.py extends matched ONNX CPU/neural
and native decode execution to all eleven original controls, including
no-price/plain, anchored compact, screened/unmasked and adapted BDQ. It
retains each original objective, horizon scaling and mask/decoder semantics.
BDQ's forward-only identity is exported directly; training gradient scaling
stays in the existing original critic. Head/runtime values use existing
atol1e-6/rtol1e-5 and exact action equality gates on tested states.
Prior frozen definitions and runtime sources were not edited.

run_assignment_price_full_fixture.py checks the exact ADP specification hash
and its frozen definitions. It runs all fifteen declared learned arms and
four heuristics on a tiny engineering plan. New original-loss DDQN candidate
updates and all original controls retain their declared arithmetic/losses.
It snapshots source/plan, final checkpoints, trained exports, paired traces,
actor/replay/anchor/optimizer diagnostics and evaluation accounting.
All final checkpoint hashes are frozen before evaluation; each graph is an
immutable trained snapshot. Reloaded online/target and optimizer states are
exercised. No checkpoint selection or controller tuning from fixture outcomes.

RELEASE BOUNDARY AND REMAINING WORK
This stage binds fixture definitions/arm list to the ADP protocol, but it does
not yet implement the entire five-fit/200-episode research execution/release.
It is deliberately impossible to launch research through the fixture runner.
The full comparison must additionally exercise multiple-fit task block/model
seed mapping, one global all75-checkpoint/export freeze before timing and
policy-quality evaluation, the full48000 timing schedule, required true
fresh-process startup reporting, all checkpoint/optimizer/replay storage and
prospective comparisons/uncertainty in a scale-reduced version of that exact
orchestration. Do not silently patch/monkeypatch this fixture into research.
Use a versioned full runner/auditor binding and a small multi-fit fixture,
then a separate source/seed/environment/launch readiness review. Research
confirmation must still be explicit and final mode remain locked.

The saved exact-overlap literature work is still incomplete; no new live
literature search occurred here. Generic DDQN+mask/congestion/branching is
not novelty. Actual learned-price/sweep benefit, competitive return/deadline,
value calibration and training stability require fresh scientific evidence.
No supervisor availability dependency is introduced for technical progress.
Word thesis and final seed reservations remain untouched. Security/trust
deferred; no cloud or charging introduced. Low-complexity contribution remains
unproven; the earlier narrow matched engineering cost pass remains preserved.
