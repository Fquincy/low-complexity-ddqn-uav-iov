LIMITS AND REQUIRED NEXT WORK
This gate has no paired research orchestration,results auditor,source-bound
research release,real demonstration training or physical-policy evaluation.
The new module's development/final CLI always rejects. Its callable helpers
are for later runner integration; they do not authorize a research run.
The caller must retain alpha,stage hashes and target-copy schedule metadata;
no new deployed buffer is added. The later auditor must verify those records.
Retention's fixed temperature can produce large gradients; the synthetic
checks do not establish stability on real demonstrations. Ridge accuracy on
withheld return targets is not guaranteed. Initial action preservation does
not guarantee retention during later TD learning or improved deadlines.
