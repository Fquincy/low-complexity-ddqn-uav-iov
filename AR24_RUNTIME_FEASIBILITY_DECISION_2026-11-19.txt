2. CONCRETE SOURCE-LEVEL OPPORTUNITIES
In ar24_prototype.decode, each task starts with its current destination's
marginal, then the candidate loop evaluates that current destination again.
For30 tasks this creates30 duplicate scores. There are up to180 sweep
marginal evaluations (old + five candidates); original mask exclusions may
reduce this. Skipping d==old preserves its already computed candidate and
the original old-on-tie preference. It is not removal of a feasible action.
All other candidates and the fixed stable visit order must stay unchanged.

The decoder computes initial scalar C through scalar(), which separately
builds CPU aggregates and radio prefixes; it then loops over placements
again to build W,X,Y and occupancy caches. The final scalar must also be
computed independently to keep the model-consistency/nonincrease check.
A new native routine can fill initial scalar accumulators AND decoder
caches in a coordinated pass, with each accumulator retaining its original
increasing-vehicle-ID update order. This can remove repeated traversal and
small-array allocation without deleting either initial or final validation.
Preserving order and output tolerance is a mandatory test, not assumed.

features(),heads(),decode(),scalar(),radio_prefix() create several small
temporary arrays. Invocation-local fixed-size scratch and native fusion
could reduce allocation/dispatch. Do not share mutable workspace across
states or rely on memoized physical features. Any frozen weights/constants
may be reused, but every input-dependent feature must be recomputed and
charged in the complete timed path.

The native marginal routine is already compiled; its existence as a Python
function does NOT establish Python call overhead inside compiled decode.
LLVM may inline it already. Do not promise a speedup merely by adding an
inline flag or count duplicate calls as measured microseconds. The neural
ONNX dispatch may dominate the remaining cost. Reference models must receive
the same graph/compiler opportunities if their execution route is changed.
