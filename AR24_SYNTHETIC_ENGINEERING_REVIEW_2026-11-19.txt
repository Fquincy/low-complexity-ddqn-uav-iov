WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
ar24_prototype.py implements the separately declared aggregate-radio design:
24-unit172-output FP32 network, signed residual costs, FP64 geometry/payload/
deadline factors, inherited compute coupling, prefix-evaluated radio scalar,
cached exact surrogate insertion marginals and one deterministic coordinate
sweep. All six new variants exist: primary,no-radio,no-deadline-weight,
homogeneous-geometry,no-CPU-coupling,no-sweep. All have17,116 parameters,
68,464 FP32 parameter bytes and20 buffer bytes. Runtime neural graph export
uses the same ONNX/native optimization opportunity as the references.
No research runner integration or training is implemented by this prototype.
