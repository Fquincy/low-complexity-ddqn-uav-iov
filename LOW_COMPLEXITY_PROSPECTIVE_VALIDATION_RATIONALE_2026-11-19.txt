PROSPECTIVE CLAIM AND VALIDATION RATIONALE
2026-11-19 | Contribution one: efficient DDQN offloading in UAV-IoV
Status: RATIONALE COMPLETE; candidate/protocol/research launch NOT released.

DECISION
Retain the approved research problem and the goal of an implemented controller
improvement. Assess storage-efficient DDQN with explicitly measured policy and
latency tradeoffs as a prospective claim, rather than promising simultaneous
dominance on every axis. This recommendation does not revise the thesis title,
approve a weaker success rule or convert any completed failure into a pass.
The original combined low-complexity requirement remains unmet.

1. DEPLOYMENT NEED AND CLAIM BOUNDARY
The system makes joint destination and UAV-movement decisions repeatedly from
current task, link/location and compressed queue information. A small online
critic can reduce persistent parameter payload, while feature extraction and
decoding can consume the saved computation. Both costs matter. The current
project has no verified target UAV memory budget, controller scheduling budget
or hardware energy measurements. Do not invent these requirements from laptop
benchmarks or claim that a small parameter array solves a demonstrated memory
shortage. A 40-slot horizon is not an inference deadline specification.

Prospective claim template, conditional on future evidence:
  An implemented [precisely named mechanism] improves [specified offloading
  policy outcome] over an equally informed same-size DDQN, using fewer online
  parameter bytes than named larger references, with complete action-selection
  cost and any policy/cost sacrifices explicitly quantified.
The blanks are unresolved; this sentence is not a present results claim.
If the same-size comparison does not establish a useful mechanism, shrinking
a standard DDQN alone is not the proposed algorithmic contribution.

Two distinct claim tracks must not be mixed:
A. Original joint efficiency target: lower storage, faster complete decisions,
   competitive return/deadlines and the frozen stability/mechanism conditions.
   No release: this remains failed/unmet under completed protocols.
B. Prospective tradeoff target: demonstrated storage reduction and a useful
   mechanism, with policy/runtime differences disclosed against ALL relevant
   controls. This could support a narrower measured contribution only after
   scientific justification, fresh validation and a novelty assessment.
No candidate, including parked AR24, is selected by this rationale.

2. MEASUREMENTS AND ESTIMANDS
Policy: episode return and deadline success under unchanged task accounting,
including unfinished tasks. Give absolute outcomes and paired differences
for each initialization; distinguish percentage points from relative change.
Let DeltaR=R_candidate-R_control and DeltaD=D_candidate-D_control on matched
task blocks. Higher is better. Do not exchange deadline losses for return
gains through a new utility function fitted to consumed results.

Persistent cost: separately record online parameter bytes, required buffers,
serialized deployment files and peak resident deployment memory if measured.
State dependencies/shared libraries and measurement scope. Report target
critic, optimizer/replay and training resources separately. Smaller FP32
parameter payload is not proof of smaller process memory or energy use.

Action cost: observation validation, all state-dependent features, neural
forward, masking, decoding and final action checks within one timed boundary.
No per-observation work may be cached outside that boundary to obtain a pass.
Match precision, threads, hardware, warmup and backend opportunities; randomize
measurement order, retain raw samples, report median/p95 and startup separately.
Use both screened and unmasked references for relevant speed claims. Report
speedup only for a matched session. Synthetic cost is not trained-policy cost
or evidence about real UAV hardware. Verify the actual evaluated deployment
policy; export discrepancies must be resolved or disclosed before claiming
equivalent deployment. Tolerances are fixed before acceptance measurements.

3. MARGINS: WHAT IS AND IS NOT JUSTIFIED
No deployment-derived policy-loss allowance or latency budget is currently
established. Consequently this rationale does NOT set a new convenient pass
threshold. Historical -2 return/-1 deadline-point margins, 70% storage caps
or 1ms p95 limits do not become independently justified merely by reuse.
Historical thresholds remain binding for their own completed experiments.

If no defensible loss allowance is available, the prospective default is to
claim an improvement only where paired evidence supports no degradation in
both policy outcomes and a positive effect in the specifically claimed one.
Zero-loss is a conservative claim boundary, not a promise it can be certified
with five fits. A tradeoff can be reported descriptively without calling it
acceptable, noninferior or Pareto-optimal. Pareto-optimality cannot be inferred
from comparison with only the finite tested controller set.

Before a future freeze, justify any nonzero margins using an explicit service
requirement or independent domain evidence. If none can be justified, retain
descriptive tradeoffs or the conservative boundary; do not backsolve a margin
from ADP's approximately 2.201-point deficit against observed-load/radio.
Similarly, parameter reduction must be reported exactly; practical importance
requires context rather than an invented memory constraint.

4. INITIALIZATIONS, TASKS AND INFERENCE
Training initialization and workload randomness are separate uncertainty
sources. Preserve all fitted models, failures and paired task blocks. Episodes
sharing a fitted controller are not independent training replications.
Report per-fit paired differences and across-initialization spread; a paired
episode bootstrap conditional on fixed fits cannot certify seed robustness.
Use a predeclared hierarchical/cluster-aware interval or an explicitly limited
descriptive analysis. Choose replication/sample size and multiplicity handling
before fresh results; five fits alone do not guarantee precise noninferiority.
If claiming simultaneous success over multiple comparators/endpoints, declare
the joint decision procedure. Do not replace an every-fit historical rule
retrospectively with an across-fit mean or select a passing initialization.
New development seeds require collision checks. Final model/task reservations
remain untouched until a separately authorized final protocol and launch.

5. VALUE STABILITY AND DDQN VALIDITY
The implementation must use a detached semigradient target:
  y = r + gamma*(1-done)*Q_target(s_next, decode(Q_online,s_next)).
The decoder, mask, approximate-greedy scope, terminal handling and same-action
critic evaluation must be explicitly specified. A single coordinate sweep is
not an exact global argmax. Any learned state-action representation must receive
the intended training gradient; target parameters must not receive updates.

Keep finite-value/gradient, sign or bound checks where mathematically warranted,
terminal error, value-scale, clipping and horizon-stratified residual reports.
Compare chosen Q to realized own-policy return-to-go with stochastic/partial-
observation limitations; separately report Bellman residuals and target lag.
Neither one-step self-consistency nor one realized return is Q-star ground truth.
Useful action ranking and calibrated scalar returns are distinct properties.
Only call calibration diagnostic in a NEW policy-only claim if the rationale
explains why the controller does not use calibrated magnitudes for confidence,
safety, cross-controller selection or service guarantees. Otherwise it remains
an acceptance endpoint. This decision must precede new outcomes. This document
does not remove calibration from an old or future frozen protocol.
Instability that affects deployed actions remains a controller reliability
problem even if a particular scalar-error endpoint is secondary.

6. CLOSEST-WORK MAP AND COMPARISON QUESTIONS
This map reuses documented saved full-method inspections; no new retrieval,
equation reproduction or exhaustive priority search was performed here.

Branching DDQN, arXiv:1711.08946: shared representation/action branches and
linear output growth exist. Include adapted BDQ and same-width plain DDQN;
branch factorization or narrow width alone is not the distinction.

Masked UAV offloading, arXiv:2407.19657: DDQN plus action filtering exists.
Supply the same justified screen to appropriate controls. Unmasked ablation
isolates screen benefit; do not claim to invent masking or transfer its
security claims into contribution one.

Vehicular DDQN scheduling, arXiv:2404.07215: queues, simultaneous requests and
server congestion exist. An observed-load/radio heuristic using the same
available information is indispensable; its current policy advantage cannot
be hidden by retaining only nearest-RSU and UAV-selective heuristics.

Hierarchical UAV-IoV offloading, arXiv:2507.05722, and FlexEdge,
arXiv:2305.01536: learned control with resource-aware allocation and execution
complexity discussions exist. A learned scorer plus deterministic allocator
is not a new mechanism merely because this combination is renamed.

Required candidate-level evidence before claiming a distinction:
exact equations/update/decoder; inputs available at decision time; asymptotic
and measured complete cost; difference from the closest existing mechanism;
one matched ablation removing ONLY that mechanism where feasible; and explicit
confounds where no pure ablation is possible. ADP's assignment-marginal/sweep
mechanisms have not demonstrated robust benefit; RQ24/AR24 remain cost-failed
untrained alternatives. No existing candidate is cleared by this map.

Future comparison core, to bind only AFTER candidate selection:
candidate; exact no-mechanism arm; same-width plain DDQN with identical screen
and training aids; compact64 and wide128 with matched implementation options;
adapted BDQ where branch-architecture novelty is asserted; observed-load/radio,
nearest-RSU and UAV-selective heuristics. Add an unmasked arm only for a stated
screen question. Equal observation information and training exposure are
required; heuristic approximations and movement restrictions must be disclosed.
Do not add arms without a distinct question or omit a stronger control to pass.

7. REVIEW DECISION AND NEXT ACTION
Rationale completed. HOLD candidate selection, implementation and research
release: no exact surviving mechanism distinction or verified runtime route
is established by this document, and deployment-based tradeoff margins remain
unjustified. This is an explicit decision, not another implied training gate.

Next bounded action: a targeted mechanism-and-feasibility assessment using
existing designs and closest method texts. Produce ONE table identifying the
equation-level distinction, necessary ablation, information parity, known
runtime bottleneck and evidence for a feasible improvement. Evaluate the
existing screened/ADP approaches before inventing another surrogate. End with
one justified candidate recommendation or an explicit HOLD if none survives;
do not automatically implement, retime, sweep widths or train to fill the gap.
The assessment should resolve one concrete mechanism question, not repeat a
general literature survey or all historical artifact audits.

GO for a subsequent candidate/protocol stage requires all of: defensible
specific distinction; feasible complete runtime reasoning; fair controls;
pre-outcome endpoint/margin rationale; and a meaningful fresh test. It is not
research launch authorization. Scope acceptance remains a departmental matter.
