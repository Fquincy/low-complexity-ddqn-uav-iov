STORAGE AND LATENCY
Every student has 67,364 FP32 parameter bytes plus 20 buffer bytes.
Thus this correction has no parameter-storage advantage over the matched
old architecture. Optimizer-containing checkpoints are training artifacts,
not deployed model storage; BC-only checkpoint sizes are not comparable
to full training checkpoint sizes as a deployment-efficiency claim.
Primary median complete action-selection latency across fits: 0.56145
to 0.70400 ms. Old baseline: 0.51595 to 0.52050 ms on this host/backend.
Timing is descriptive; no predeclared speed gate or cross-device claim.
These observations do not establish a new low-complexity improvement.

WHAT THE CONTROLS ESTABLISH
The BC-only arm starts from the independently verified same BC-end
weights as the primary. Its better final policy in every fit shows that
the specified DDQN continuation degraded policy relative to stopping
after BC in this study. The cause is not yet established.
Demonstration-using arms substantially outperform the ordinary no-demo
small controller here. That useful observation is distinct from proving
the proposed centered-linear correction improves the existing baseline.
The stronger teacher remains better than every learned arm on both means.
Do not select a winning fit or retrospectively promote a secondary arm.
Stronger compact64/wide128/BDQ/full-DQfD comparisons remain deferred.
