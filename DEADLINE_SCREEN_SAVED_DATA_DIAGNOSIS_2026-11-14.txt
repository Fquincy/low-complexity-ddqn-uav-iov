DEADLINE SCREEN: SAVED DEVELOPMENT TRACE DIAGNOSIS
Date: November14,2026

OUTCOME
Saved evidence localizes the remaining policy failures to different remote
choices across initializations. Impossible-local execution is no longer the
observed failure mode. The price critic also systematically predicts returns
more negatively than those realized by its greedy policy. This diagnosis
does not establish the learning cause or justify an untested remedy.

EVIDENCE AND CONSISTENCY
720 saved evaluation traces:3 screened controllers x5fits x48task episodes;
864,000 task records across policy traces, with576,000 paired task comparisons
(primary vs each of2 controls, all5fits). Every JSON/NPZ input hash matched
its original run manifest. Relevant15checkpoint hashes match the frozen set.
Task IDs, cycles, input bits, deadlines, vehicle IDs and arrival times match
across each paired task block. Paired on-time outcome differences reproduce
all original deadline contrasts. Destination/failure counts and return residual
summaries reconcile with the audited run. No simulator stepping, optimizer
updates, new tasks, timing or fitting. Historical final seeds remain untouched.

LOCAL SCREEN: OBSERVED SUCCESS AND LIMIT
All15screened fits executed zero provably locally infeasible tasks and had
zero local deadline misses in these720traces. The original screening ablation
gain is retained. This is an empirical observation under these workloads,
not a guarantee that the conservative screen prevents queue-related local
misses in other conditions or that any retained remote is deadline-feasible.

Primary predictions are systematically too negative under its own recorded
greedy trajectories in all5fits. No-price predictions are also too negative
in all5fits, so this is not exclusive to the explicit price term. Plain24
has mixed signed errors and is also not uniformly calibrated. Primary early
episode bias is large even though terminal p95 error passed the frozen gate.

On saved trajectories, e_t=Q_t-r_t-Q_(t+1), Q_40=0, obeys exactly
Q_t-G_t=sum_(k=t)^39 e_k (verified to<1e-10). Thus persistent stepwise
inconsistency accumulates through the horizon. This is a mathematical
decomposition, not proof of an optimizer, loss, target-copy or representation
cause. Reported target-based residuals use the same future executed greedy
actions and frozen target network; they are not replay-time training losses.
Primary online-minus-target means range+.501..+1.612: the target is on average
more negative. This association alone cannot establish target lag caused bias.
Exact saved gradient/loss/target/residual/clipping distributions from the
completed independent audit are included per fit for all3controllers.

PRICE DECOMPOSITION: WHAT IT DOES AND DOES NOT EXPLAIN
Frozen primary networks were evaluated only on their already saved states.
Chosen price cost is about0.162%,0.327%,0.227%,0.669%,0.784% of total
predicted per-remaining-step cost on average in fits0..4. It is a small share
of the chosen-value magnitude; this does not establish a negligible effect
on greedy action rankings, especially near ties. Neural unary/bias terms
and future training visitation differ between separately trained ablations.
Do not attribute the whole global calibration bias to this small explicit
additive term, nor infer deleting it from the trained primary will reproduce
a trained no-price controller. Descriptive decomposition uses batch inference;
it does not replace recorded batch-one Q, audited predictions or thresholds.

RECOMMENDED NEXT DECISION
Keep the full low-complexity DDQN goal and this failed combined decision intact.
Do not immediately launch more training or choose only the best initialization.
The evidence supports considering one prospective controller-design brief
addressing remote service/link interactions and horizon-wide return consistency.
Any mechanism must be defined against observed information and computational
cost, then distinguished from equally informed small DDQN alternatives.
The present evidence does not justify merely increasing training time, changing
price strength, adding stronger masks or modifying losses without declaration.

Before implementation, decide whether to retain a separately justified price
mechanism or simplify the architecture; screened anchored plain24 is a useful
control but its fit2deadline/return failure prevents treating it as a ready
replacement. Write the equations, intended benefit, information limits and
bounded comparison amendment first. Fresh disjoint development seeds would
be required for any later scientific run. Keep final confirmation separate.
Literature comparison and supervisor agreement remain open. No thesis claim
is promoted by this diagnostic analysis; security/trust remains deferred.
